# T8. Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation

Selection guide: each line shows what the method solves and when NOT to use it (first variant; open the card for every variant's full text).

## 8.1 Organisational Change

- [Cultural Analysis](../methods/cultural-analysis.md) — Analyzes organizational corporate culture (Deal & Kennedy, Handy, Hofstede, Johnson & Scholes Cultural Web), reducing change rejection caused by cultural incompatibility. *Not for:* For micro-level technical bug fixes with zero cultural or human impact.
- [Lewin's Change Model](../methods/lewins-change-model.md) — Provides a 3-stage framework (Unfreeze -> Transition -> Freeze) to guide change implementation, reducing change rejection and reversion to old habits. *Not for:* When managing continuous micro-iterations where a permanent frozen state is intentionally avoided.
- [Kotter's 8 Steps](../methods/kotters-8-steps.md) — Provides a comprehensive 8-step framework (Urgency, Coalition, Vision, Communicate, Remove obstacles, Short-term wins, Build, Anchor) for leading successful organizational change. *Not for:* For small, routine micro-tasks or isolated technical maintenance with no organizational reach.

## 8.2 People and Learning

- [SARAH Model](../methods/sarah-model.md) — Maps the emotional journey individuals experience during major change (Shock -> Anger -> Rejection -> Acceptance -> Hope), guiding empathetic change support strategies. *Not for:* For minor, unnoticeable system tweaks that trigger no emotional impact on workers.
- [Learning Cycle](../methods/learning-cycle.md) — Explains how individuals learn skills through a 4-stage cycle (Concrete Experience, Reflection, Abstract Concepts, Testing) and 4 Learning Styles (Activist, Reflector, Theorist, Pragmatist). *Not for:* When delivering fully automated machine-executable script updates requiring zero human learning.
- [Conscious Competence Model](../methods/conscious-competence-model.md) — Tracks skill acquisition across 4 stages (Unconscious Incompetence -> Conscious Incompetence -> Conscious Competence -> Unconscious Competence), guiding user training and support. *Not for:* For automated system-to-system interfaces requiring no human operator learning.

## 8.3 Benefits and Outcome Evaluation

- [Benefits Management](../methods/benefits-management.md) — Manages a project continuously against a formal Benefits Plan and Benefits Dependency Framework, maximizing the probability that business benefits are actually achieved. *Not for:* In small, non-investment experimental research spikes where benefits cannot be structured.
- [Benefits Realisation](../methods/benefits-realisation.md) — Evaluates whether business benefits were actually achieved post-implementation, identifying corrective actions if shortfalls occur and capturing lessons learned for future projects. *Not for:* Immediately post-go-live before the system has had sufficient operational running time to generate measurable benefits data.
- [Lessons Learned](../methods/lessons-learned.md) — Prevents repeating past mistakes and amplifies project successes by systematically compiling, documenting, and applying team retrospectives. *Not for:* When team trust is completely absent and facilitation cannot guarantee a psychological safe, constructive discussion.
- [Runtime Requirements Monitoring](../methods/runtime-requirements-monitoring.md) — Reduces system failure when real-world environment assumptions are violated or change dynamically after system deployment. *Not for:* For simple static systems operating in fully controlled, unchanging environments where assumptions never fail.
