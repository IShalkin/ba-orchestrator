# PALM (Business Goals for Architecture)

`T4 Modelling and Analysis › 4.6 Architecture Analysis`

**Also described as:** Pedigreed Architecture Analysis Method for Business Goals (PALM)

## Problem Solved

Reduces uncertainty regarding business goals and aligns architectural decisions directly with business motivations.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Elicitation

## Input Artifacts

Business strategy documents, corporate vision statements, executive mandates.

## Output Artifacts

Prioritized business goal scenarios, identified quality attribute requirements linked to business goals.

## Participants & Roles

Facilitator, Software Architect, Executive/Business Stakeholders, Product Manager.

## Prerequisites

Availability of business leaders capable of articulating strategic business goals.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Present PALM overview.
2. Present business drivers and context.
3. Elicit business goals using standard categories.
4. Consolidate and refine business goals into structured business goal scenarios.
5. Prioritize business goal scenarios.
6. Map prioritized business goals to specific quality attribute requirements.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What are the growth, financial, market, and process goals of the organization? How does this system contribute to meeting those organizational business goals?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Eliciting a business goal to 'reduce annual operating costs by 30%', which maps to a quality attribute requirement for energy efficiency and resource auto-scaling.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Traceability between business goal scenarios and corresponding quality attribute scenarios; stakeholder consensus on goal priorities.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Confusing technical features with business goals; focusing only on short-term project objectives rather than organizational business goals.

## When NOT to Use

Projects where business goals are already explicit, fixed, and clearly mapped to technical requirements.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Quality Attribute Workshop (QAW), Utility Tree Construction, Balanced Scorecard.

## Supported Decisions

Scope decisions, architecture investments, build vs. buy decisions, risk mitigation priorities.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI agents can analyze strategic corporate artifacts to draft candidate business goal scenarios. Human oversight is mandatory to confirm strategic priorities and business alignment.
