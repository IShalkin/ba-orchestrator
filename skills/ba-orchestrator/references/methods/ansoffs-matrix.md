# Ansoff's Matrix

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.1 External Environment and Market Analysis`

**Also described as:** Ansoff's matrix

## Problem Solved

Structures product and market growth strategies, reducing uncertainty when identifying alternatives for strategic expansion.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Strategy Definition / Option Evaluation

## Input Artifacts

SWOT Analysis, market research, product capability documentation.

## Output Artifacts

2x2 Growth Strategy Matrix (Market Penetration, Market Development, Product Development, Diversification).

## Participants & Roles

BA, Product Manager, Business Owner, Marketing Director.

## Prerequisites

Completed SWOT analysis and organizational growth objectives.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Evaluate existing products in existing markets (Market Penetration).
2. Evaluate existing products in new markets (Market Development).
3. Evaluate new products in existing markets (Product Development).
4. Evaluate new products in new markets (Diversification).
5. Assess risks and select strategic growth option.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Can we grow by selling more existing products to existing customers? Can we find new customer segments or regions for existing products? Can we build new features/products for current customers?

## Minimal Realistic Example

An established UK training provider decides to sell its existing course portfolio in European markets (Market Development strategy).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Clear risk-return profiling of each quadrant option, backed by objective market feasibility research.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Choosing high-risk diversification without leveraging existing core competencies or existing customer relationships.

## When NOT to Use

When analyzing purely internal process optimizations or non-growth compliance projects.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Ansoff's Box, SWOT Analysis, Boston Box, Value Proposition Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Scope definition, product roadmap decisions, market expansion strategy.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Generative AI simulates market responses and risk profiles for proposed expansion strategies across new geographic or demographic segments.
