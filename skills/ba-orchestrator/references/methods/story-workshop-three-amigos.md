# Story Workshop (Three Amigos)

`T5 Requirements Specification › 5.1 User Stories and Story Mapping`

**Also described as:** Story Workshop / Deep-Dive Elaboration (The Three Amigos)

## Problem Solved

Prevents late-stage delivery blockers, scope creep, and inaccurate estimates caused by developers building software without deep, shared understanding of behavior details and edge cases.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Analysis / specification / prioritisation

## Input Artifacts

Draft user story, UI wireframes/sketches, persona context, technical architecture context.

## Output Artifacts

Right-sized user stories (1–3 days of dev work), refined acceptance criteria, explicit technical/testing notes, updated estimates.

## Participants & Roles

The Three Amigos: Product Representative (Product Owner/BA/UX), Developer(s), Tester.

## Prerequisites

Selected story from the release backlog; candidate UI sketches/wireframes.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Gather the Three Amigos in front of a whiteboard.
2. Walk through the user story flow using wireframes/drawings.
3. Discuss edge cases, business rules, and error handling.
4. Speak in concrete examples.
5. Split stories that are too big into smaller 'cupcakes' (Good-Better-Best).
6. Document bulleted acceptance criteria and confirmation steps on the board/card.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"What specific data is entered here?", "What happens if the service is unavailable?", "Can we thin this story down to a Good-Enough version?", "What will the tester check to verify this?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA, developer, and tester discuss 'View Movie Info'. They realize it's too big and split it: 'Good' (view title, poster, preview), 'Better' (add synopsis and member ratings), 'Best' (add trivia and discussion board).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Right-sized stories executable in 1–3 days; consensus among all three roles; explicit test cases defined.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Including the entire team (large crowd meeting torture); one person reading a specification document aloud while others disengage; skipping tester involvement; estimating without shared understanding.

## When NOT to Use

High-level strategic roadmap planning; simple bug fixes with obvious technical execution.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Backlog Grooming, Backlog Refinement, Fishbowl Collaboration, BDD Elaboration.

## Supported Decisions

Sprint backlog readiness, story splitting, acceptance test definition, effort estimation.
