# Activity Sampling

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.4 Domain, Document and Data Research`

**Also described as:** Sampling

## Problem Solved

Measures transaction times and time allocation objectively across business tasks, reducing reliance on subjective staff estimates.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Elicitation / Analysis

## Input Artifacts

Activity sampling sheets, list of task activity codes, observation schedule.

## Output Artifacts

Activity sampling summary sheets, moving average graphs, task time metrics.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Sampler), Observed Staff Members, Line Manager.

## Prerequisites

Agreement from management and staff/unions, established random or fixed sampling intervals.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Explain purpose to managers and staff.
2. Define standard activity codes.
3. Establish sampling intervals (e.g., every 15 mins).
4. Record observed activity code for each worker at each interval.
5. Summarize daily results and calculate moving averages.
6. Stop sampling when moving averages stabilize.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What specific activity is worker X performing at this precise sample moment?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA conducts a 3-day activity sampling exercise across 7 bank call center staff, discovering that 34% of time is spent amending account details.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Stabilization of the calculated moving average across daily samples; sufficient sample size.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Sampling at predictable intervals that coincide with fixed breaks; stopping sampling before moving averages stabilize; worker behavior distortion.

## When NOT to Use

When detailed qualitative reasons behind tasks are needed, or when sample populations are hostile to measurement.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Special-Purpose Records, Document Analysis, Observation.

## Supported Decisions

Business case cost-benefit calculation, process bottleneck identification, resource capacity planning.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Automated system-event logging software continuously samples user system interactions to generate time-allocation metrics automatically.
