# Leavitt's Diamond

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.3 Current State, Capabilities and Gaps`

## Problem Solved

Analyzes proposed organizational changes holistically across four interdependent components, preventing project failure caused by isolated technological or structural changes.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Analysis / Strategy Implementation / Change Management

## Input Artifacts

Proposed change initiatives, process models, IT architecture plans, organizational charts.

## Output Artifacts

Impact analysis matrix across Task, Structure, People, and Technology.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Change Manager, IT Lead, Business Process Owner, HR SME.

## Prerequisites

An initial change proposal originating in at least one component (e.g., new technology).

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify the primary driver component for change (e.g., Technology).
2. Analyze required changes to Tasks/processes.
3. Analyze required changes to People (skills, staffing, training).
4. Analyze required changes to Structure (roles, reporting lines).
5. Ensure all 4 components are balanced and harmonized.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

How does this technology change affect daily tasks? What new skills do people need? Does this require restructuring teams or lines of communication?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Introducing a workflow system (Technology) leads to redefined event-booking procedures (Task), staff training (People), and merging two administrative departments (Structure).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Demonstrated consideration of interdependencies across all four components so no component is modified in isolation.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Treating IT system deployment purely as a technical upgrade without adjusting human skills, tasks, or reporting structures.

## When NOT to Use

For minor bug fixes or minor localized routine maintenance tasks.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

POPIT Model, McKinsey 7-S, Impact Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Implementation planning, change impact scope, training and organizational restructuring plans.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Agentic impact models predict downstream disruption to tasks and required skill upgrades whenever a technology component change is introduced.
