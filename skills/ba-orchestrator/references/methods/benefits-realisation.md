# Benefits Realisation

`T8 Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation › 8.3 Benefits and Outcome Evaluation`

**Also described as:** Benefits realisation

## Problem Solved

Evaluates whether business benefits were actually achieved post-implementation, identifying corrective actions if shortfalls occur and capturing lessons learned for future projects.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Manage Change / Benefits Realisation / Post-Project Review

## Input Artifacts

Business Case, Benefits Plan, Evaluation criteria, Operational performance metrics.

## Output Artifacts

Benefits Realisation Report, Corrective Action Plan, Organizational Lessons Learned log.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Project Sponsor, Benefit Owners, Line Managers, Post-Project Reviewer.

## Prerequisites

Completed project implementation and sufficient operational running time.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Review original Business Case and Benefits Plan evaluation criteria.
2. Collect post-implementation operational performance data.
3. Compare actual realized benefits against target metrics.
4. Identify shortfalls and investigate root causes.
5. Formulate corrective action plans (e.g. extra training, publicity) to salvage benefits.
6. Publish Benefits Realisation Report and archive organizational lessons learned.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Did we achieve the expected benefits outlined in the business case? What is the actual metric value today vs the baseline? If benefits were missed, why? What corrective action can salvage them?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A post-implementation review of a customer support portal reveals online usage is lower than targeted; the review identifies lack of customer awareness, initiating a publicity campaign to salvage the benefit.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Objective measurement of actual post-implementation metrics against pre-set evaluation criteria; actionable salvage plans for missed benefits.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Failing to conduct post-implementation reviews; conducting 'witch hunts' instead of objective evaluations; measuring benefits too early before operational stabilization.

## When NOT to Use

Immediately post-go-live before the system has had sufficient operational running time to generate measurable benefits data.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Benefits Management, Post-Implementation Review (PIR), Cost-Benefit Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Corrective action intervention, future project selection/estimation refinement, organizational learning.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Post-implementation telemetry AI automatically audits live operational KPIs against business case targets, drafting Benefits Realisation Reports and highlighting shortfalls.
