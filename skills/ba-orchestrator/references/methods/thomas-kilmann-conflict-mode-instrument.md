# Thomas-Kilmann Conflict Mode Instrument

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.3 Engagement, Conflict and Negotiation`

**Also described as:** The Thomas–Kilmann conflict mode instrument

## Problem Solved

Identifies five conflict resolution modes (Avoiding, Accommodating, Competing, Compromising, Collaborating) based on assertiveness and cooperativeness, reducing unhelpful conflict handling.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Stakeholder Management

## Input Artifacts

Stakeholder analysis outputs, conflict observations.

## Output Artifacts

Conflict resolution strategy assessment.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Facilitator/Negotiator), Conflicting Stakeholders.

## Prerequisites

Identification of a disagreement or conflict situation between stakeholders.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Assess the assertiveness and cooperativeness of conflicting parties.
2. Identify current conflict modes (Avoiding, Accommodating, Competing, Compromising, Collaborating).
3. Evaluate the significance of the issue.
4. Guide parties toward an appropriate mode (e.g., Collaborating for high-value win-win, Compromising for fast balance).

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Is this issue critical enough to justify deep collaboration? Can we find a creative win-win option? Is a compromise acceptable here?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA recognizes two leads taking a 'Competing' stance over UI layouts, guiding them toward 'Collaborating' by brainstorming creative new layout alternatives.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Selection of a conflict mode appropriate to the strategic importance and relationship needs of the situation.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Assuming 'Competing' vs 'Accommodating' are the only choices; relying habitually on 'Avoiding' for major conflicts, causing festering resentment.

## When NOT to Use

When technical specifications are governed by strict non-negotiable legal regulations.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Principled Negotiation, BATNA, CATWOE.

## Supported Decisions

Conflict resolution strategy, requirement trade-offs, negotiation approaches.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI negotiation assistants analyze participant phrasing in chat/email to classify active conflict modes and suggest de-escalation strategies.
