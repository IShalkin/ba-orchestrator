# 4As Communication Model

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.5 Business Case`

**Also described as:** ‘4As’ communication model

## Problem Solved

Provides a 4-part planning structure (Aim, Audience, Arrangement, Appearance) for developing effective presentations and business documents.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Evaluate Options / Present Business Case

## Input Artifacts

Presentation topics, stakeholder list, venue details.

## Output Artifacts

Structured Presentation Plan / Document Layout (Aim, Audience analysis, Arrangement outline, Appearance specifications).

## Participants & Roles

BA (Presenter/Author), Target Audience / Stakeholders.

## Prerequisites

Need to communicate business analysis findings, proposals, or business cases.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Define Aim (why is presentation required, desired outcome).
2. Analyze Audience (who are they, what are their aims/expectations, background).
3. Plan Arrangement (structure: Tell 'em what you'll tell 'em -> Tell 'em -> Tell 'em what you told 'em).
4. Design Appearance (presenter attire, slide readability, font consistency, visual style).

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What is my specific aim? Who is in my audience and what do they want? How should I arrange the logical flow? How should the slides/document visually appear?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA uses 4As to structure a project update: setting Aim (secure extra budget), analyzing Audience (CFO/Sponsor needs), Arrangement (Problem -> Solution -> ROI), and Appearance (clean visual charts).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Explicit alignment between presenter aim and audience needs; clear logical arrangement; readable visual design.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Ignoring audience expectations; creating unreadable slides with bad fonts/colors; lacking a structured opening/closing arrangement.

## When NOT to Use

For casual, informal 1-on-1 chats where formal communication planning is unnecessary.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Business Case Presentation, Business Case Report Creation.

## Supported Decisions

Presentation structure approval, document format design, communication strategy execution.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI communication assistants evaluate presentation drafts against 4As criteria, highlighting unreadable fonts, missing audience hooks, or weak logical flows.
