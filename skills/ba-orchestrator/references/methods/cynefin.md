# Cynefin

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.1 Problem and Scope Framing`

## Problem Solved

Categorizes business situations into 4 contexts (Simple, Complicated, Complex, Chaotic) to guide the selection of appropriate leadership, analysis, and decision-making approaches.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Analysis / Option Evaluation

## Input Artifacts

Problem descriptions, stakeholder viewpoints, situational investigation data.

## Output Artifacts

Categorized Situational Context (Simple, Complicated, Complex, Chaotic, or Disorder) and selected analytical/development approach.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Sponsor, Executive Leadership, Project Manager.

## Prerequisites

Investigation into the cause-and-effect predictability of the business situation.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Analyze situation predictability and cause-and-effect visibility.
2. Categorize as Simple (Sense-Categorize-Respond / Best Practice), Complicated (Sense-Analyze-Respond / Good Practice), Complex (Probe-Sense-Respond / Emergent Practice), or Chaotic (Act-Sense-Respond / Novel Practice).
3. Identify if the situation sits in Disorder.
4. Select matching BA approach (e.g., Waterfall for Simple/Complicated; Agile/Prototyping for Complex; Pragmatic fast intervention for Chaotic).

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Is cause-and-effect clear to everyone? Are there 'known unknowns' requiring expert analysis? Are there 'unknown unknowns' requiring experimentation? Is immediate action needed to stop panic?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA categorizes a disruptive new market entry as 'Complex', recommending an Agile prototyping approach rather than a rigid upfront waterfall specification.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Accurate assessment of situational complexity, preventing over-simplification of complex problems.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Applying rigid best-practice waterfall specifications to Complex problems; over-analyzing Chaotic situations instead of taking immediate action; remaining in Disorder.

## When NOT to Use

When the project approach and domain complexity are already thoroughly understood and fixed.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Sensemaking, Risk Analysis, Gap Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Project lifecycle selection (Agile vs Waterfall), leadership style selection, decision-making approach.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Decision-support AI evaluates project risk parameters and historic success factors to classify new change initiatives into Cynefin quadrants.
