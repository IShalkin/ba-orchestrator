# Walk-Through

`T7 Validation and Acceptance › 7.1 Reviews and Inspections`

## Problem Solved

Establishes a common understanding of requirements and identifies quality flaws through an informal, author-guided group session.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Validation

## Input Artifacts

Requirements document / specifications draft

## Output Artifacts

Logged quality flaws, meeting minutes, shared understanding notes

## Participants & Roles

Author, Reviewers/Inspectors, Minute-taker, (Optional) Moderator/Reader

## Prerequisites

Requirements document distributed to participants in advance.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Hand out requirements documents to reviewers for initial reading.
2. Convene the walk-through session.
3. The author (or reader) guides participants step-by-step through the requirements.
4. Author provides background rationale, context, and alternatives considered.
5. Reviewers raise questions and point out flaws.
6. Minute-taker logs all identified quality issues.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"What was the rationale behind this rule?", "Is this step clear to the development team?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA walks developers and testers through a new feature specification, explaining business rules and logging clarification points raised by developers.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Achieving team-wide shared understanding; logging of explicit defects and ambiguities.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Author defensiveness; lack of prior reading by reviewers leading to superficial feedback.

## When NOT to Use

When formal compliance sign-off or contract-bound rigorous validation is mandatory (use formal Inspection).

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Inspection, Commenting

## Supported Decisions

Draft approval, clarification of implementation scope.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Virtual assistants present sections of specifications and auto-summarize developer queries and identified ambiguities.
