# Stakeholder Wheel

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.1 Stakeholder Identification`

**Also described as:** The stakeholder wheel

## Problem Solved

Provides a comprehensive categorization checklist across 8 internal and external groups, preventing omission of critical external stakeholders.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Identification

## Input Artifacts

Project context description, business domain overview.

## Output Artifacts

Categorized Stakeholder Map covering Owners, Managers, Employees, Regulators, Suppliers, Partners, Customers, and Competitors.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Sponsor, Key Business Leads.

## Prerequisites

Understanding of the organizational operating model and business domain.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Review the 8 categories of the Stakeholder Wheel.
2. Identify Owners (shareholders, ministers).
3. Identify Managers and Employees.
4. Identify Regulators and Standards bodies.
5. Identify Suppliers and Partners.
6. Identify Customers and Competitors.
7. Compile comprehensive stakeholder list.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Who regulates this business? Who supplies raw materials/services? Who are our delivery partners? Who are our customer groups and competitors?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA on a conference-booking project uses the wheel to identify venue suppliers, keynote speakers, external event partners, and regulatory safety bodies.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Systematic evaluation of all 8 categories of the wheel, ensuring external stakeholders are not neglected.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Focusing exclusively on internal managers/employees while ignoring regulators, partners, or suppliers.

## When NOT to Use

When analyzing a tiny internal micro-task with no external interactions or organizational dependencies.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Stakeholder Nomination, Background Research, Power/Interest Grid.

## Supported Decisions

Stakeholder management planning, communication strategy, risk identification.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Web-scraping AI agents automatically search regulatory registers, partner listings, and market landscapes to populate external wheel categories.
