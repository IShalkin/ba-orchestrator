# Learning Cycle

`T8 Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation › 8.2 People and Learning`

**Also described as:** The learning cycle

## Problem Solved

Explains how individuals learn skills through a 4-stage cycle (Concrete Experience, Reflection, Abstract Concepts, Testing) and 4 Learning Styles (Activist, Reflector, Theorist, Pragmatist).

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Manage Change / People Change / Training / Workshop Facilitation

## Input Artifacts

Training needs analysis, stakeholder profiles, workshop plans.

## Output Artifacts

Tailored Training Curriculum, Balanced Workshop Facilitation Strategy.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Facilitator/Trainer), Business Users / Workshop Attendees.

## Prerequisites

Planning of training programs or stakeholder workshops.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Understand the 4 cycle stages (Concrete Experience, Observation & Reflection, Abstract Concepts, Testing).
2. Recognize participant Learning Styles (Activist - jump in; Reflector - analyze data; Theorist - assimilate logic; Pragmatist - practical application).
3. Design balanced training/workshops catering to all 4 styles.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What learning style dominates this stakeholder group? How can we balance jump-in exercises (Activists) with quiet reflection (Reflectors) and practical application (Pragmatists)?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Designing a workshop for mixed styles: combining a fast Post-it exercise (Activists/Pragmatists) with pre-circulated reading materials and quiet reflection time (Reflectors/Theorists).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Training/workshop design incorporates activities for all 4 learning cycle stages and styles.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Designing workshops exclusively for Activists (e.g. non-stop loud brainstorming), alienating Reflectors and Theorists; presenting pure theory without practical exercises.

## When NOT to Use

When delivering fully automated machine-executable script updates requiring zero human learning.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Kolb's Cycle, Honey & Mumford's Learning Styles, Conscious Competence Model.

## Supported Decisions

User training curriculum design, workshop facilitation technique selection, change adoption planning.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Adaptive AI learning platforms monitor user interaction habits to deliver dynamically tailored training modules matching individual learning styles.
