# Power/Interest Grid

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.2 Stakeholder Analysis and Mapping`

**Also described as:** Power/interest grid

## Problem Solved

Categorizes stakeholders based on their level of power and interest, reducing communication failures and resistance by establishing tailored management strategies.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Analysis

## Input Artifacts

Identified stakeholder list, organizational context, CATWOE outputs.

## Output Artifacts

Power/Interest Grid Matrix (2x2 or 3x3), baseline stakeholder management categories.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Project Sponsor, Project Manager.

## Prerequisites

Completed stakeholder identification exercise.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Estimate each stakeholder's level of power/influence (Low/Medium/High).
2. Estimate each stakeholder's level of interest (Low/Medium/High).
3. Plot stakeholders onto the grid.
4. Assign management strategies: High Power/High Interest (Manage Actively), High Power/Low Interest (Keep Satisfied/Watch), Low Power/High Interest (Keep Informed/Onside), Low Power/Low Interest (Ignore/Minimal Effort).
5. Re-evaluate grid periodically.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

How much power does this stakeholder have to influence or block the project? How interested are they in the project outcome? How might their power or interest change?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Operational clerks are plotted as Low Power/High Interest ('Keep Informed') while the Finance Director is plotted as High Power/Low Interest ('Keep Satisfied').

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Accurate assessment of actual influence vs formal title; regular review to catch shifting power/interest levels.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Assuming operational staff have no power and ignoring them (leading to collective resistance/unions); treating the grid as a static one-off artifact.

## When NOT to Use

When working with a single business user on a localized personal productivity tool.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Social Network Analysis, RASCI Charts, CATWOE, Stakeholder Management Plan.

## Supported Decisions

Stakeholder communication plans, risk management, project governance structure.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Sentiment analysis engines monitor stakeholder emails and project communications to automatically adjust power/interest grid coordinates.
