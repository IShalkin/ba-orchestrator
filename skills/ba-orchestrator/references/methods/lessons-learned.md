# Lessons Learned

`T8 Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation › 8.3 Benefits and Outcome Evaluation`

## Problem Solved

Prevents repeating past mistakes and amplifies project successes by systematically compiling, documenting, and applying team retrospectives.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Planning / Solution Evaluation / change management

## Input Artifacts

Project deliverables, performance metrics, issue logs, process assessments.

## Output Artifacts

Lessons Learned report / Retrospective log (successes, improvement opportunities, root causes, actionable recommendations).

## Participants & Roles

Facilitator, BA, Project Manager, Agile Team, Key Stakeholders.

## Prerequisites

Completion of a project phase, iteration, or milestone in a psychological safe environment.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Schedule a retrospective meeting at a key milestone or iteration end.
2. Set an open, non-blaming tone focused on continuous improvement.
3. Identify what worked well (successes to reinforce).
4. Identify what didn't work well (problems, variances, failures).
5. Perform root cause analysis on identified challenges.
6. Formulate concrete, actionable recommendations for future work and store in an accessible repository.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"What went well during this iteration that we should continue?", "What caused our review cycle to take longer than estimated?", "What should we do differently next time?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

In a sprint retrospective, the team identifies that missing wireframes caused developer rework; they agree to require wireframe sign-offs prior to sprint planning.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Atmosphere remains constructive without personal blame; recommendations are actionable and integrated into organizational process assets.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Turning sessions into finger-pointing 'blame games'; documenting lessons learned in reports that are archived and never read again.

## When NOT to Use

When team trust is completely absent and facilitation cannot guarantee a psychological safe, constructive discussion.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Alternatives/Complements include Retrospectives, Root Cause Analysis, Process Analysis, and Collaborative Games.

## Supported Decisions

BA process improvements, future project estimation adjustments, team working agreement updates.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI agents can summarize retrospective meeting notes across multiple teams, flagging recurring organizational bottlenecks and suggesting process rule adjustments.
