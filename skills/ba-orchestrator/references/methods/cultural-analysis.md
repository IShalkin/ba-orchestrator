# Cultural Analysis

`T8 Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation › 8.1 Organisational Change`

**Also described as:** Cultural analysis

## Problem Solved

Analyzes organizational corporate culture (Deal & Kennedy, Handy, Hofstede, Johnson & Scholes Cultural Web), reducing change rejection caused by cultural incompatibility.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Manage Change / Organisational Change

## Input Artifacts

Organizational context, cultural observations, leadership style data, Cultural Web components.

## Output Artifacts

Cultural Analysis Report / Cultural Web Matrix (Paradigm, Stories, Rituals, Symbols, Org Structures, Control Systems, Power Structures).

## Participants & Roles

BA, Change Manager, HR Director, Executive Sponsor.

## Prerequisites

Understanding of informal organizational norms, stories, and power dynamics.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Analyze central Paradigm (core assumptions/mission).
2. Audit Stories and myths.
3. Audit Rituals and Routines.
4. Audit visual Symbols and dress codes.
5. Audit formal/informal Organizational Structures.
6. Audit Control Systems and metrics.
7. Map Power Structures.
8. Evaluate cultural gap between current and required culture.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What is the core paradigm? What stories are told to newcomers? What rituals are valued? Where does real power lie? How does our proposed change fit this culture?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Analyzing Virgin Atlantic using the Cultural Web: defining Paradigm ('profitable airline people love'), un-earthing Richard Branson stories, flat structures, informal dress (Symbols), and visible punctuality metrics (Controls).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Holistic audit covering both subjective (stories, rituals) and objective (controls, structures) cultural dimensions; clear gap identification between current and required culture.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Treating culture as an irrelevant soft factor; attempting to impose changes that directly contradict the un-addressed core organizational paradigm.

## When NOT to Use

For micro-level technical bug fixes with zero cultural or human impact.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Cultural Web, Handy's Cultural Types, Hofstede's Dimensions, POPIT Model.

## Supported Decisions

Change management strategy, organizational redesign, leadership engagement approach, communication strategy.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

NLP agents analyze internal communications, employee surveys, and news archives to construct initial Cultural Web models and flag cultural risk areas.
