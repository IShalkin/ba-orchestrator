# Customer Journey Mapping

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.8 Prototypes and Scenarios for Elicitation`

**Also described as:** Customer Experience Analysis & Journey Mapping

## Problem Solved

Prevents poor user adoption and customer dissatisfaction by mapping emotional pain points and needs across end-to-end customer interactions.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Elicitation

## Input Artifacts

User Personas, Stakeholder Interviews, User Observation Logs, Support Tickets.

## Output Artifacts

Customer Journey Map, Touchpoint Identification Chart, Experiential Non-Functional Requirements, Service Blueprints.

## Participants & Roles

Business Analyst, UX Designer, Customer/End User, Marketing Lead, Service/Support Lead.

## Prerequisites

Defined customer personas and identified core customer goals/transactions.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Select a target customer persona and goal.
2. Map the end-to-end timeline of touchpoints across three phases: Before, During, and After.
3. Identify customer actions, feelings, and pain points at each touchpoint.
4. Highlight emotional highs/lows and information needs.
5. Brainstorm requirements to eliminate friction and enhance convenience/delight.
6. Derive functional and non-functional requirements to support the improved journey.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"What is the customer experiencing before touching our product?", "What information does the customer need at this specific touchpoint?", "What causes frustration or anxiety here?", "How can we make this step effortless or delightful?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

Mapping a refrigerator repair journey across 'Before' (fridge breaks, finding repair service), 'During' (waiting for engineer, getting quote, fixing), and 'After' (restocking, follow-up), deriving requirements for automated SMS arrival windows and digital quotes.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Journey map reflects genuine end-user perspective rather than internal organizational view; pain points directly mapped to newly defined requirements.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Mapping internal business processes instead of the customer's actual journey; assuming the customer's emotional state without observation or research.

## When NOT to Use

Internal backend system components with no direct or indirect human user touchpoints.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Empathy Mapping, Storyboarding, Contextual Inquiry / Apprenticing, Service Blueprinting.

## Supported Decisions

UX design priority, customer-facing feature scope, service improvement investments.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

LLMs trained on customer feedback and sentiment data can draft initial customer journey maps, highlighting common emotional friction points across touchpoints for BA review.
