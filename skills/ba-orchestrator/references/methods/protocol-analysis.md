# Protocol Analysis

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.5 Interviews, Questionnaires and Individual Techniques`

**Also described as:** Protocol analysis

## Problem Solved

Uncovers cognitive decision-making processes and tacit knowledge by having users give a running verbal commentary while performing a task.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Elicitation

## Input Artifacts

Task checklist, recording equipment (optional), process documentation.

## Output Artifacts

Verbal transcript, decision trees/flowcharts, documented business rules.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Observer/Listener), Subject Matter Expert (Performative Commentator).

## Prerequisites

Selection of an articulate SME performing a decision-intensive task; consent for commentary/recording.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Select decision-heavy task.
2. Brief SME to speak thoughts aloud continuously while working.
3. Observe and record the running commentary and actions.
4. Gently prompt SME if they go silent.
5. Transcribe and analyze cognitive steps and decision rules.
6. Model as flowchart or decision table.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Please keep talking and tell me what you are looking at right now? What decision are you making? What rule made you choose that option?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A bank loan officer speaks aloud while evaluating a loan application, explaining subtle red flags they look for that are not written in official manuals.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Successful capture of cognitive decision logic and tacit heuristics that were previously undocumented.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Using harsh questioning that unnerves the subject; performing protocol analysis on long, exhausting tasks without breaks; assuming commentary is 100% complete.

## When NOT to Use

For simple, highly repetitive physical tasks requiring no cognitive decision-making, or with non-articulate subjects.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Observation, Shadowing, Decision Tables, Task Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Business rules specification, expert system logic design, decision process automation.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Voice-recognition AI captures and parses the verbal commentary stream directly into candidate business rule specifications and decision trees.
