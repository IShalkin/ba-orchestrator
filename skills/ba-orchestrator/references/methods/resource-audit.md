# Resource Audit

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.3 Current State, Capabilities and Gaps`

## Problem Solved

Identifies internal resource capability, highlights core competencies, and uncovers weaknesses that could prevent or undermine business change.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Analysis

## Input Artifacts

Financial reports, asset inventories, HR skill profiles, IT architecture diagrams, organizational charts.

## Output Artifacts

Resource Audit profile categorized by Physical, Financial, Human, Reputation, and Know-how resources.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Business Owner, HR Manager, Finance Manager, Operations SME.

## Prerequisites

Access to organizational financial, physical, and human resource data.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Audit Physical resources (land, buildings, equipment).
2. Audit Financial resources (assets, cash flow, credit).
3. Audit Human resources (staff numbers, skills, motivation).
4. Audit Reputation (brand perception, goodwill).
5. Audit Know-how (data, information systems, IP).
6. Categorize findings into organizational strengths and weaknesses.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Do we have sufficient physical space and equipment? Is the business financially stable? Are staff skilled and motivated? What is our brand reputation? How effectively do we capture intellectual know-how?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A consulting firm audits its resources, identifying high consultant motivation (Human Strength) but fragmented, unintegrated ad-hoc IT systems (Know-how Weakness).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Factual verification of resource existence, availability, and quality against objective benchmarks.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Underestimating the time and effort required for a thorough audit; ignoring intangible assets like organizational know-how and culture.

## When NOT to Use

In small-scale, rapid hypothesis testing where resource constraints are well understood or negligible.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Resource Analysis, SWOT Analysis, McKinsey 7-S, Capability Modelling.

## Supported Decisions

Feasibility decisions, build vs. buy, investment decisions, resource allocation.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Automated data-mining agents summarize internal HR skills databases, asset management registers, and financial metrics into a consolidated resource dashboard.
