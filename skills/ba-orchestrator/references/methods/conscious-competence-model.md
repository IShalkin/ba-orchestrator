# Conscious Competence Model

`T8 Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation › 8.2 People and Learning`

**Also described as:** The conscious competence model

## Problem Solved

Tracks skill acquisition across 4 stages (Unconscious Incompetence -> Conscious Incompetence -> Conscious Competence -> Unconscious Competence), guiding user training and support.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Manage Change / People Change / Training / Support

## Input Artifacts

Training plans, role descriptions, SARAH model outputs.

## Output Artifacts

Competency Transition Plan (Awareness, Training/Coaching, Practice/Mentoring, Mastery).

## Participants & Roles

BA, Trainers, Mentors, Business Users.

## Prerequisites

Introduction of new business processes or IT systems requiring new skills.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Unconscious Incompetence: sensitively raise awareness of new required skills.
2. Conscious Incompetence: provide formal training and support to alleviate fear/anxiety.
3. Conscious Competence: provide coaching/mentoring as users practice (accepting short-term productivity dips).
4. Unconscious Competence: celebrate mastery and capture new improvement ideas.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What stage of competence are users currently at? How do we support them through Conscious Incompetence without causing panic? Have we allowed for short-term productivity dips during Conscious Competence?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Supporting users during a CRM rollout: acknowledging anxiety at Stage 2, providing intensive desk-side coaching during Stage 3 (where productivity temporarily dips), until system use becomes second nature at Stage 4.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Demonstrated progression of users through to Unconscious Competence; planned allowance for temporary productivity dips during Stage 3.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Expecting instant Stage 4 mastery immediately post-training; treating Stage 2 anxiety as incompetence; criticizing users during the Stage 3 learning dip.

## When NOT to Use

For automated system-to-system interfaces requiring no human operator learning.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

The Learning Cycle, SARAH Model, Training Needs Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Training schedule design, post-go-live support planning, productivity impact estimation.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI performance-support assistants provide real-time in-app guidance to users during Stage 3 (Conscious Competence) to accelerate progression to mastery.
