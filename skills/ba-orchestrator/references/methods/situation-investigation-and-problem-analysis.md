# Situation Investigation and Problem Analysis

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.1 Problem and Scope Framing`

## Problem Solved

Uncovers underlying root causes rather than symptoms of business problems, clarifies ambiguous problem statements, and resolves conflicting stakeholder perspectives.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Elicitation / Discovery / Analysis

## Input Artifacts

Initial problem requests, background documents, company reports, organisation charts, current process documentation.

## Output Artifacts

Problem statement, Rich picture, Mind map, Fishbone diagram, Context diagram, project scope/ToR.

## Participants & Roles

Sponsor, Business Owner, BA, SME, Business Staff.

## Prerequisites

Initial business problem statement or change request, access to stakeholders and documentation.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Investigate the current situation and problem/opportunity.
2. Understand the strategic context.
3. Identify and articulate business needs.
4. Define the root problem using diagnostic techniques.
5. Define the scope of the business change project.
6. Formulate terms of reference (OSCAR) or rationale for rejection.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Why do you do this? What value does it add? What is the root cause? What is happening in practice versus what is documented? What is the real problem to be solved?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Investigating declining juice sales and rising complaints at Simply Juices to reveal that aggressive telesales targets and a poorly designed website (not product quality) were the root causes.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Verification that root causes rather than symptoms are identified, alignment with strategic context, stakeholder consensus on problem statement.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Leaping to premature software solutions without investigating the problem, confusing symptoms with root causes, accepting verbal statements without quantitative validation.

## When NOT to Use

When the issue is purely technical with zero business impact, or the root cause is completely known and verified.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Complementary: PESTLE, Five Forces, CATWOE, Activity Sampling, Surveys, Interviews, Workshops.

## Supported Decisions

Scope, project initiation, project rejection.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI tools can perform automated document analysis, sentiment analysis, and initial data clustering to highlight potential anomalies or recurring complaint topics.
