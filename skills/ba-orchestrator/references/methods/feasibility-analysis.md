# Feasibility Analysis

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.4 Solution Options and Feasibility`

**Also described as:** Feasibility analysis

## Problem Solved

Evaluates shortlisted options across Business, Technical, and Financial dimensions, reducing the risk of selecting unviable, unaffordable, or technically impossible options.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Evaluate Options / Shortlist Options

## Input Artifacts

Options longlist, business strategy, technical architecture, budget constraints.

## Output Artifacts

Feasibility Assessment Report evaluating shortlisted options against Business, Technical, and Financial criteria.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Business Owner, Technical Architect, Finance Manager, HR Lead.

## Prerequisites

Shortlist of candidate options.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Evaluate Business feasibility (Strategic fit, market conditions, timeliness, culture, org fit, competencies, legal).
2. Evaluate Technical feasibility (Availability, reliability, maintainability, performance, security, scalability, skills, compatibility).
3. Evaluate Financial feasibility (Budget, funds, ROI, cash flow, payback).
4. Document feasibility findings for each option.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Does this option fit our business strategy? Is it culturally acceptable? Is the technology reliable and scalable? Do we have the technical skills? Is it within budget?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Evaluating a proposed software package: finding high business fit, but revealing technical infeasibility due to incompatibility with legacy databases and lack of internal support skills.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Comprehensive assessment across all three dimensions (Business, Technical, Financial) backed by factual evidence.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Evaluating only technical feasibility while ignoring organizational culture and staff competency fit; presenting over-optimistic financial projections.

## When NOT to Use

During initial creative brainstorming where feasibility filtering would stifle idea generation.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Force-Field Analysis, PESTLE, SWOT, Investment Appraisal.

## Supported Decisions

Option shortlisting, business case recommendation, risk identification.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Automated feasibility evaluators cross-check option parameters against enterprise architecture rules, regulatory databases, and financial constraints.
