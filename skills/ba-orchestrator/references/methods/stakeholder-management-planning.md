# Stakeholder Management Planning

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.3 Engagement, Conflict and Negotiation`

**Also described as:** Stakeholder management planning

## Problem Solved

Establishes structured, ongoing action and communication plans for each stakeholder, reducing project resistance and maintaining executive buy-in.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Stakeholder Management

## Input Artifacts

Power/Interest Grid, RASCI Chart, CATWOE outputs, stakeholder list.

## Output Artifacts

Stakeholder Management Plan table (Name, Power, Interest, Issues/Interests, Current Attitude, Desired Support, Desired Role, Desired Actions, Messages, Communications).

## Participants & Roles

BA, Project Sponsor, Project Manager, Change Manager.

## Prerequisites

Completed stakeholder analysis (Power/Interest, CATWOE, RASCI).

## Step-by-Step Procedure

List stakeholder names and roles.
2. Record Power and Interest levels.
3. Document key issues and interests.
4. Classify Current Attitude (Champion, Supporter, Neutral, Critic, Opponent, Blocker).
5. Define Desired Support, Role, and Actions.
6. Formulate tailored Messages and Communication actions.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What is this stakeholder's current attitude? What support do we need from them? What key messages will resonate with them? How frequently should we communicate?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Creating a plan for Marketing Director Steven Lewis (High Power/High Interest / Champion), scheduling bi-weekly meetings to review web marketing objectives.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Tailored communication strategies aligned with power/interest grid positions; actionable communication schedules.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Treating the management plan as a static document; sending generic mass-emails to high-power stakeholders instead of personalized engagement.

## When NOT to Use

In trivial, single-person projects with no wider organizational impact.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Stakeholder Map, Power/Interest Grid, Communication Plan.

## Supported Decisions

Communication strategy, governance schedule, risk mitigation, change management intervention.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

CRM-style stakeholder AI engines log engagement interactions, track attitude changes, and trigger automated reminders for scheduled communications.
