# MOST Analysis

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.2 Strategy, Goals and Measures`

**Also described as:** MOST analysis

## Problem Solved

Examines strategic direction alignment, ensuring tactics and operational efforts directly support higher-level mission and objectives, reducing strategic misalignment and conflicting priorities.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Analysis / Change Management

## Input Artifacts

Corporate strategy documents, mission statements, business plans, stakeholder interviews.

## Output Artifacts

MOST Matrix/Statement (Mission, Objectives, Strategy, Tactics), identified organizational strengths and weaknesses.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Sponsor, Executive Leadership, Business Owner.

## Prerequisites

Availability or definition of the corporate mission and strategic plans.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify and document the overall business Mission.
2. Identify measurable Objectives aligned to the Mission.
3. Identify medium-to-long term Strategies designed to achieve Objectives.
4. Identify short-term detailed Tactics that execute the Strategy.
5. Analyze alignment, clarity, definition, and staff commitment across all four levels.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What is our business rationale/direction? What specific targets prove we met the mission? What strategic plans deliver those targets? What daily tactical activities execute those strategies?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A retail company sets Mission 'Be top online fashion seller', Objective 'Increase online sales by 20%', Strategy 'Target youth market', and Tactic 'Launch TikTok marketing campaign'.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Vertical alignment: every tactic maps to a strategy, every strategy to an objective, and every objective to the mission.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Assuming a published MOST equates to staff buy-in; defining vague objectives without clear targets; decoupling daily operations from strategic plans.

## When NOT to Use

When performing pure low-level technical bug fixing or minor isolated operational changes with no strategic impact.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

VMOST, SWOT analysis, Balanced Business Scorecard, Resource Audit.

## Supported Decisions

Scope definition, strategy validation, change prioritization, project initiation.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

LLM agents analyze corporate communications and backlog items to detect misalignment between low-level task executions and strategic MOST goals.
