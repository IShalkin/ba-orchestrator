# Benchmarking and Market Analysis

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.1 External Environment and Market Analysis`

## Problem Solved

Reduces uncertainty about competitive standing, market trends, and organizational efficiency gaps by comparing internal performance against external baselines or market demands.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / elicitation / analysis / change management

## Input Artifacts

Existing process metrics, published industry benchmarks, competitor reports, RFI responses, market research data.

## Output Artifacts

Gap analysis report, market research summary, best practice recommendations, RFI evaluation.

## Participants & Roles

Sponsor, Business Owner, BA, Domain SME, Marketing/Strategy Lead.

## Prerequisites

Clear definition of internal operational focus areas and access to reliable market/competitor data sources.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify specific operational processes or market questions to evaluate.
2. Identify industry leaders, competitors, or best-in-class standard baselines.
3. Collect data via surveys, RFIs, market research, or site visits.
4. Analyze gaps between internal performance and external best practices/customer expectations.
5. Identify market trends, customer segment preferences, and competitor strengths/weaknesses.
6. Develop proposals or strategy adjustments based on findings.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"Who are the leaders in our industry and what practices make them successful?", "What features do competitors offer that we lack?", "What are the emerging market trends and customer buying preferences?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

A retail bank conducts a benchmark study on customer onboarding time against top 3 fintech competitors, discovering an average 3-day gap, which drives a digital transformation initiative.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Data sources must be credible and recent; market segments must be properly differentiated to avoid misleading comparisons.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Copying competitor practices blindly without accounting for internal context; spending excessive time/budget on research without actionable outcomes.

## When NOT to Use

When seeking novel, ground-breaking innovations (benchmarking only reproduces existing practices), or when fast action is required on immediate hypotheses without market research budget.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Complemented by SWOT Analysis, Competitive Analysis, Focus Groups, and Survey/Questionnaire.

## Supported Decisions

Market entry/exit decisions, capability investment priorities, solution selection, change strategy definition.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

LLM agents can scrape and analyze web data, competitor offerings, and industry reports to generate automated market summaries and gap matrices, flagged for analyst review.
