# Development Case Customization

`T6 Prioritisation and Requirements Management › 6.3 Organisation, Baselines and Reuse`

## Problem Solved

Reduces process inefficiency, unnecessary bureaucracy, or project failure caused by misaligned process ceremony.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Change management / project initiation

## Input Artifacts

Project Risk Profile, Team Experience Levels, Organizational Culture/Standards.

## Output Artifacts

Development Case (tailored process specification).

## Participants & Roles

Project Manager, Lead BA/System Analyst, Architect, QA Manager.

## Prerequisites

Project initiation and team setup.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Assess project criticality, size, dispersion, and team experience to determine required ceremony level.
2. Select applicable activity groups, modeling templates, and artifacts.
3. Classify artifact and step usage as Mandatory, Recommended, or Suggested for each workflow/phase.
4. Document process guidelines and tool assignments in the Development Case.
5. Review and update Development Case iteratively between project milestones.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What is the risk if we omit this artifact? What level of ceremony does our stakeholder community require?

## Minimal Realistic Example

High-risk medical project classifies Use Cases and Test Cases as 'Mandatory'; low-risk internal tool classifies Sequence Diagrams as 'Suggested'.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Process audit, team consensus, alignment between project risk profile and document overhead.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Enforcing maximum high ceremony on small experienced teams or zero ceremony on large distributed high-risk projects.

## When NOT to Use

Single-person, trivial 1-week tasks where process overhead exceeds work effort.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Agile Development Frameworks, RUP Tailoring, ISO 9001 Process Mapping.

## Supported Decisions

Process compliance, resource allocation, project governance.
