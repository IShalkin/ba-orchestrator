# Development Process Requirements

`T5 Requirements Specification › 5.6 Requirements Documents and Phrasing`

## Problem Solved

Mitigates high requirement uncertainty or scope volatility by specifying the development and change management process rather than fixed product features.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

specification

## Input Artifacts

Project charter, risk management plan, agile development methodology outlines.

## Output Artifacts

Contractual process requirements (e.g., prototyping iterations, change management rules, unit pricing formulas).

## Participants & Roles

BA, project manager, customer, supplier.

## Prerequisites

High project uncertainty where fixed-price feature specification is unfeasible.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify volatile or highly uncertain system requirement areas.
2. Define required iterative development processes (e.g., 3 prototype iterations).
3. Establish change management procedures and bi-weekly review boards.
4. Define unit pricing structures (e.g., price per additional screen or Function Point).
5. Incorporate process requirements into the contract.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

How will we evaluate change requests? What unit pricing formula will govern unforeseen additions?

## Minimal Realistic Example

R1: System development shall use iterative development based on prototypes. R2: Supplier shall deliver additional screens at $X per screen.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Process audit, joint review sign-offs, adherence to change board protocols.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Committing to fixed-price development without locking down either features or process pricing rules.

## When NOT to Use

Simple, well-known COTS purchases with fixed, non-negotiable scopes.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Tasks & Support, Open Target / Open Metric Requirements.

## Supported Decisions

Contract pricing structure, vendor engagement model, change management governance.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Monitor change request logs automatically and calculate impact estimates based on contractual unit pricing rules.
