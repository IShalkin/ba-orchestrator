# Value Proposition Analysis

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.2 Strategy, Goals and Measures`

**Also described as:** Value proposition analysis

## Problem Solved

Analyzes customer perspectives on value across Timing, Financial, and Quality categories for Owners, Partners, and End Customers, reducing misalignment between business processes and customer expectations.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Analyse Needs / Organisation Modelling

## Input Artifacts

Customer feedback, market research, business strategy, CATWOE outputs.

## Output Artifacts

Value Proposition Matrix mapping customer types against value categories.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Marketing Manager, Product Manager, Business Owner.

## Prerequisites

Identification of key customer groupings (Owners, Partners, End Customers).

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify target customer types (Owners/Managers, Partners/Resellers, End Customers).
2. Analyze value expectations across Timing, Financial, and Quality categories for each type.
3. Map value proposition criteria into a matrix.
4. Evaluate current and proposed business processes against the matrix.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Why do customers choose us over competitors? What do Owners expect (dividends, reports)? What do Partners expect (discounts, service)? What do End Customers expect (price, speed, quality)?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Mapping supermarket end-customers who prioritize 'high quality and reasonable price', contrasting with owners who demand 'timely financial returns and high profit margins'.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Clear distinction between the differing value propositions of Owners, Partners, and End Customers; alignment of business processes to value criteria.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Focusing solely on internal cost-cutting (Owner value) while degrading product quality/speed (End Customer value).

## When NOT to Use

For purely technical internal infrastructure maintenance projects with no customer-facing or commercial dimensions.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Value Chain Analysis, Value Streams, Personas, Organization Diagram.

## Supported Decisions

Business process redesign priorities, service level target setting, product positioning.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Customer analytics AI processes review streams and feedback metrics to dynamically map and update customer value proposition matrices.
