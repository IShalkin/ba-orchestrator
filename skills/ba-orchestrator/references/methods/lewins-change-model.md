# Lewin's Change Model

`T8 Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation › 8.1 Organisational Change`

**Also described as:** Kurt Lewin’s model of organisational change

## Problem Solved

Provides a 3-stage framework (Unfreeze -> Transition -> Freeze) to guide change implementation, reducing change rejection and reversion to old habits.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Manage Change / Organisational Change

## Input Artifacts

Force-Field Analysis, Change strategy, POPIT assessment.

## Output Artifacts

Change Implementation Plan structured into Unfreeze, Transition, and Freeze stages.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Change Manager, Sponsor, Line Managers, Business Users.

## Prerequisites

Definition of required business changes and force-field assessment.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Unfreeze stage: overcome inertia, dismantle old mindsets, communicate urgency and evidence for change.
2. Transition stage: execute changes, support staff through uncertainty, handle errors with sensitivity.
3. Freeze stage: anchor new working practices, establish new stability, reinforce behaviors so staff do not revert to old habits.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

How will we unfreeze current habits and demonstrate the need for change? How will we support people during transition confusion? How will we freeze the new process so staff don't revert?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Implementing a public smoking ban: Unfreezing via health evidence campaigns -> Transitioning on the implementation date with support -> Freezing as non-smoking becomes the new socially accepted norm.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Explicit activities planned and executed for all three stages; demonstrated stability (permanency) achieved during the Freeze stage.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Skipping the Unfreeze stage and forcing change on unprepared staff; failing to Freeze the new state, allowing staff to revert to legacy habits.

## When NOT to Use

When managing continuous micro-iterations where a permanent frozen state is intentionally avoided.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Kotter's 8-Step Change, Force-Field Analysis, SARAH Model.

## Supported Decisions

Change management plan design, user readiness evaluation, post-implementation stabilization.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Change analytics AI tracks user adoption metrics during Transition to advise managers when an organization is ready to move to the Freeze stage.
