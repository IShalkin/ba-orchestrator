# Benefits Categorisation

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.5 Business Case`

**Also described as:** Benefits categorisation

## Problem Solved

Classifies business benefits into four distinct categories (Financial, Quantifiable, Measurable, Observable), providing a nuanced framework beyond simple tangible/intangible labels.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Evaluate Options / Prepare Business Case / Benefits Management

## Input Artifacts

Cost-Benefit Analysis, Business Case, stakeholder input.

## Output Artifacts

Categorized Benefits List (Financial, Quantifiable, Measurable, Observable) with defined evaluation mechanisms.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Benefit Owner, Finance Lead, Sponsor.

## Prerequisites

Identification of proposed project benefits.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Review proposed benefits.
2. Categorize as Financial (provable in advance in cash terms).
3. Categorize as Quantifiable (provable in advance in non-cash metrics, e.g. lives saved).
4. Categorize as Measurable (measurable after the event, e.g. website sales increase).
5. Categorize as Observable (assessed via expert judgment/agreed criteria, e.g. staff morale).
6. Define evaluation mechanisms for each.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Is this benefit provable in cash terms in advance (Financial)? Is it provable in advance using non-cash metrics (Quantifiable)? Can we measure it before and after (Measurable)? Must it be judged by experts using criteria (Observable)?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Categorizing benefits for a level-crossing safety project: 'Closing crossings' is Quantifiable (saves X lives/yr based on statistics), but assigning cash value is separate.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Appropriate categorization based on proof availability and measurement timing; clear pre-defined evaluation mechanism for every category.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Attempting to force fake financial values onto purely observable benefits; failing to establish baseline metrics before project execution for measurable benefits.

## When NOT to Use

When preparing a simple informal rough estimate with no formal benefits management framework.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Cost-Benefit Analysis, Benefits Dependency Framework, Benefits Realisation.

## Supported Decisions

Business case justification, benefits management plan design, post-project evaluation criteria definition.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI analytics engines match proposed benefits against historic enterprise tracking data to classify them accurately into the four categories.
