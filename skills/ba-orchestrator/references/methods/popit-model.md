# POPIT Model

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.3 Current State, Capabilities and Gaps`

**Also described as:** The POPIT™ model

## Problem Solved

Provides an holistic framework for identifying required business changes across four key dimensions, preventing single-dimensional (e.g. IT-only) project failure.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Analysis / Change Management / Specification

## Input Artifacts

Gap analysis results, business strategy, requirements catalogue, system architecture.

## Output Artifacts

POPIT Change Assessment across Processes, Organization, People, and Information/Technology.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Sponsor, Change Manager, HR Lead, Enterprise Architect.

## Prerequisites

Identification of proposed business or system changes.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Evaluate required changes to Business Processes.
2. Evaluate required changes to Information & Technology systems.
3. Evaluate required changes to People (skills, roles, communication).
4. Evaluate required changes to Organization (structure, management, resources).
5. Align all four areas to ensure coherent implementation.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

If we change this process, how does it affect IT systems? What training do people need? Does the organizational management structure support this?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Redesigning a customer service process (Process) requires upgraded CRM screens (IT), staff training (People), and revised team supervisory roles (Organization).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

All four quadrants are populated and cross-checked for mutual alignment and completeness.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Implementing technical changes without updating business processes or providing staff training.

## When NOT to Use

For trivial bug fixes or micro-level task edits that carry no broader organizational impact.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Four-view model, McKinsey 7-S, Leavitt's Diamond, Impact Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Business case option design, change impact scope, target operating model specification.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

GenAI evaluates change request tickets to flag missing impact dimensions across People, Process, IT, or Organizational structure automatically.
