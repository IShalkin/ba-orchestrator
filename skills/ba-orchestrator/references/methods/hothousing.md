# Hothousing

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.6 Group and Creative Sessions`

## Problem Solved

Accelerates requirement clarification and solution prototyping through an intensive 3-day off-site workshop with competing teams, significantly shortening delivery timescales.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Define Requirements / Elicitation / Analysis / Solution Design

## Input Artifacts

Business problems, Use case scenarios, PIR 'handshake' metrics.

## Output Artifacts

Selected Working Prototype, PIR Handshake document (measurable targets & 90-day objectives), Cost-Benefit Analysis, Metrics.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Facilitators, Cross-functional Teams (Developers, Business Users, Partners, Customers), Judges/Sponsors.

## Prerequisites

Off-site venue, technical infrastructure, clear business problem statement, 3-day commitment.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Day One: Present business problem, teams brainstorm approaches, present outlines, score/rank.
2. Day Two: Teams build working prototypes with direct user feedback, score/rank.
3. Day Three: Fine-tune prototypes, evaluate final solutions, select winning prototype.
4. Establish 90-day release timebox and PIR handshake.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

How can we solve this business problem in 3 days? What is the core working prototype we can build? What measurable targets define success for our 90-day release?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A retail bank holds a 3-day off-site hothouse where 3 cross-functional teams compete to build a mobile loan application prototype, selecting the winning app for a 90-day rollout.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Working prototype delivered within 3 days; explicit PIR handshake defining measurable targets for the subsequent 90-day release timebox.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Failing to secure off-site isolation leading to work distractions; lacking real business decision-makers during the event; over-scoping beyond the 3-day limit.

## When NOT to Use

For routine, minor system maintenance or when key business users and developers cannot commit to 3 days off-site.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Hackathons, Agile Prototyping, Facilitated Workshops, Timeboxing.

## Supported Decisions

Solution architecture selection, 90-day release scope approval, business case metrics sign-off.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI coding assistants accelerate hothouse prototype generation, allowing teams to build complex working features in hours during the 3-day sprint.
