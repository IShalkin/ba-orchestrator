# Business Process Improvement

`T4 Modelling and Analysis › 4.1 Business Processes and User Work`

## Problem Solved

Reduces operational inefficiencies, bottlenecks, hand-off errors, waste, and poor customer experiences.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Analysis / Specification / Change Management

## Input Artifacts

Enterprise value streams, 'as is' process models, customer journey maps, performance metrics.

## Output Artifacts

'To be' business process models, task definitions, gap analysis report, revised job roles, process measurement targets.

## Participants & Roles

Process Owner, BA, SME, End User/Actor, Solution Architect.

## Prerequisites

Documented 'as is' business processes, clear understanding of customer value expectations and organisational targets.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Model existing ('as is') processes.
2. Analyse hand-offs, bottlenecks, and wastes (TIMWOODS).
3. Define required ('to be') processes.
4. Perform gap analysis between 'as is' and 'to be'.
5. Define process measures (time, quality, cost).
6. Identify implementation actions (e.g., POPIT changes, automation).
7. Ensure alignment with supporting IT systems.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Why is this step necessary? Where are the hand-offs? Where do queues or bottlenecks form? Does this task add value to the customer? How can we eliminate waste?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Redesigning an order-fulfillment process by combining stock-checking and reservation steps, reducing processing time from 2.5 days to 4 hours.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Process efficiency gains, reduction in TIMWOODS wastes, alignment with value propositions, verification via simulation or walkthroughs.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Optimising an individual task at the expense of the end-to-end process (sub-optimisation), ignoring hand-off delays, paving over bad processes with IT automation.

## When NOT to Use

When the business domain is entirely new and requires greenfield process engineering rather than incremental improvement.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

SIPOC, Value Stream Mapping, Value Chain Analysis, Six Sigma (DMAIC), Customer Journey Mapping, Lean.

## Supported Decisions

Process architecture, job role design, resource allocation, automation scope.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Agentic AI can monitor live process execution data to automatically map 'as is' variants, detect bottlenecks, and propose optimal task routing.
