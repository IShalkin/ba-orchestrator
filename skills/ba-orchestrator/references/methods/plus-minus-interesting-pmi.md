# Plus-Minus-Interesting (PMI)

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.4 Solution Options and Feasibility`

## Problem Solved

Evaluates proposed solution alternatives objectively by categorizing positive, negative, and uncertain repercussions.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Negotiation / Analysis

## Input Artifacts

Solution alternatives (e.g., from CAF), decision criteria

## Output Artifacts

PMI table, categorized impact report

## Participants & Roles

Requirements Engineer / Moderator, Conflicting Stakeholders

## Prerequisites

Defined solution alternatives to a requirement conflict.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Take a proposed requirement solution alternative.
2. List all positive consequences under the 'Plus' column.
3. List all negative consequences under the 'Minus' column.
4. List uncertain or neutral elements under the 'Interesting' column.
5. Investigate 'Interesting' items further to classify them as Plus or Minus.
6. Compare total scores/impacts across alternatives to select the best option.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"What are the explicit advantages of this option?", "What negative side effects does this choice introduce?", "What consequences are still uncertain?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

A team evaluates switching to open-source map data: Plus = lower cost; Minus = no vendor SLA; Interesting = potential community customization options.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Clear classification of consequences; thorough investigation of all 'Interesting' items.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Failing to convert 'Interesting' items into clear positives/negatives through research.

## When NOT to Use

When quantitative decision criteria (e.g., monetary weights) are strictly required.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Decision Matrix, Consider-All-Facts

## Supported Decisions

Alternative selection, conflict resolution.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

LLM agents quickly populate PMI tables by analyzing past project post-mortems and technical documentation.
