# Social Network Analysis

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.2 Stakeholder Analysis and Mapping`

**Also described as:** Social network analysis

## Problem Solved

Maps informal relationships and communication pathways between individuals, reducing disruption caused by missing informal influencers and information brokers.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Analysis

## Input Artifacts

Interviews, observation notes, email flow tracking, surveys.

## Output Artifacts

Social Network Map identifying Central Connectors, Boundary Spanners, Information Brokers, and Peripheral Specialists.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Analyst), Departmental Staff.

## Prerequisites

Understanding of informal workplace communication flows.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Gather interaction data via interviews, observation, or email tracking.
2. Map nodes (people) and directional interaction lines.
3. Identify Central Connectors (high interaction hubs).
4. Identify Boundary Spanners (cross-departmental bridges).
5. Identify Information Brokers (information conduits).
6. Identify Peripheral Specialists (isolated experts).
7. Formulate communication strategies based on network roles.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Who do you go to first when you need information from another department? Who really holds this team together? Who possesses specialized knowledge?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Mapping a manufacturing firm reveals Vicky (a junior clerk) as the Central Connector binding Design, Sales, and Production together.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Accurate mapping of actual informal advice and communication channels rather than official organigram hierarchy lines.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Assuming official management hierarchy reflects actual informal communication channels; ignoring peripheral specialists leading to lost expert knowledge.

## When NOT to Use

In small 2-person projects or highly rigid automated environments with zero human interaction.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Power/Interest Grid, Stakeholder Map, Ethnographic Studies.

## Supported Decisions

Change agent selection, communication routing, key-person risk management, training distribution.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Enterprise collaboration analytics (e.g. Slack/Teams graph analysis) automatically generate dynamic social network maps.
