# McKinsey 7-S

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.3 Current State, Capabilities and Gaps`

## Problem Solved

Ensures holistic organizational alignment across 7 core areas during business change, reducing implementation failures caused by misaligned soft/hard factors.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Analysis / Strategy Implementation / Change Management

## Input Artifacts

Strategy definition, organizational structure, HR policies, business process maps, cultural audits.

## Output Artifacts

7-S Alignment Audit Report, impact analysis, change initiative recommendations.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Sponsor, Change Manager, HR Lead, Enterprise Architect.

## Prerequisites

A defined organizational strategy or proposed business change proposition.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Analyze Shared Values (superordinate goals).
2. Assess Hard elements: Strategy, Structure, Systems.
3. Assess Soft elements: Style, Staff, Skills.
4. Evaluate alignment and interdependencies between all 7 elements.
5. Identify misalignment gaps and required change interventions.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Do our internal structures support the new strategy? Are staff skills aligned with new system needs? Does management style fit our core shared values?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A company shifting to high-volume low-cost sales uses 7-S to reveal that its highly paid, high-touch senior sales staff (Staff/Skills) conflict with the new low-cost strategy.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

All 7 dimensions must demonstrate mutual reinforcement and explicit alignment with the shared values.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Focusing exclusively on hard elements (Structure, Systems) while ignoring soft elements (Style, Staff, Skills, Shared Values).

## When NOT to Use

For isolated, localized software tweaks that have zero impact on staff, structure, or management culture.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

POPIT Model, Leavitt's Diamond, Cultural Web.

## Supported Decisions

Target Operating Model design, organizational restructuring, change management strategy.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Agentic systems monitor employee sentiment, process adherence, and skill distribution to report real-time alignment scores against corporate strategy.
