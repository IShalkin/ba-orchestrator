# CATWOE

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.2 Stakeholder Analysis and Mapping`

## Problem Solved

Uncovers and analyzes underlying stakeholder worldviews, values, and assumptions, reducing hidden conflicts, contradictory requirements, and strategic misalignment.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Analysis

## Input Artifacts

Stakeholder interviews, background research, stakeholder list.

## Output Artifacts

CATWOE Analysis breakdown (Customer, Actor, Transformation, Worldview, Owner, Environment), Root Definition statement.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Key Stakeholders (Sponsors, Department Leads, SMEs).

## Prerequisites

Identification of key stakeholders whose perspectives need to be evaluated.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify the stakeholder's Worldview (W) - their underlying beliefs and values.
2. Define the core Transformation (T) process that fulfills W.
3. Identify the Customer (C) benefiting or affected by T.
4. Identify the Actors (A) performing T.
5. Identify the Owner (O) with authority over T.
6. Identify Environmental (E) constraints surrounding T.
7. Assemble into a concise Root Definition statement.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What is this stakeholder's core belief about the business purpose (W)? What core transformation process fulfills this (T)? Who is the recipient (C)? Who carries out the work (A)? Who can stop it (O)? What constraints exist (E)?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A retail operations director's CATWOE defines W as 'fashionable clothes must be inexpensive', T as 'sell cheap trendy clothes', C as 'young consumers', and O as 'the board'.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Clear derivation of Transformation directly from the Worldview; explicitly validated Root Definition signed off by the stakeholder.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Omitting the Worldview (W) and jumping straight to process details; defining generic actors like 'staff' without role specificity; drafting Root Definitions without stakeholder validation.

## When NOT to Use

For low-level, non-contentious technical coding tasks where stakeholder worldviews are identical and unproblematic.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

VOCATE, PARADE, Business Activity Modelling (BAM), Root Definition.

## Supported Decisions

Business activity model baseline, conflict resolution strategy, gap analysis alignment, requirements prioritization.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

LLM agents analyze interview transcripts across different department leads to construct draft CATWOE matrices and highlight conflicting worldviews automatically.
