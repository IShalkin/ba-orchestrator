# Product Scope Formula

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.1 Problem and Scope Framing`

## Problem Solved

Defines explicit boundaries between project and product, mitigating scope creep and establishing clear decision criteria for project approval.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Specification / Analysis / Discovery

## Input Artifacts

Confirmed real problem statement, product vision, stakeholder list, business case inputs, risk/constraint logs.

## Output Artifacts

Product Scope Statement / Charter Section detailing problem, vision, acceptance criteria, functional goals, justifications, and constraints.

## Participants & Roles

Business Analyst (author), Problem Owner, Sponsor, Project Manager.

## Prerequisites

Completed and confirmed real problem statement and product vision.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Draft the core statement using the structured formula template.
2. Insert the specific system/process solution scope.
3. Incorporate defined functional goals and desired vision.
4. Specify the acceptance criteria that prove problem resolution.
5. Attach the confirmed real problem statement.
6. Add optional parameters: target stakeholders, strategic justifications, business/product constraints, and business risks.
7. Review the formulated product scope with the problem owner and project manager.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

• What functional goals must be accomplished to achieve the vision?
• What specific acceptance criteria will prove the problem is solved?
• What business or regulatory constraints limit this product?
• What strategic justification justifies this scope?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Formula applied: 'This vendor management process will accomplish automated term calculations resulting in 100% correct vendor payments which will pass 0% payment discount forfeiture solving the inflexible vendor database problem on behalf of Accounts Payable to realize decreased costs within Sarbanes-Oxley constraints considering key staff turnover risks.'

## Quality Criteria & Verification

The product scope must clearly distinguish product capabilities from project execution work, be directly traceable to corporate strategy, and contain measurable acceptance criteria.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Confusing product scope (features/functions) with project scope (schedule, staffing, execution tasks); omitting business constraints.

## When NOT to Use

In extremely minor operational defect fixes where scope boundaries are completely self-evident and localized.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Project Charter, Vision and Scope Document, Solution Scope, User Story Mapping.

## Supported Decisions

Project charter approval, budget allocation, release planning, scope change control.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI agents can auto-generate structured scope draft statements by parsing conversation transcripts from discovery sessions against the scope formula template, highlighting missing constraints or unstated risks for the human analyst.
