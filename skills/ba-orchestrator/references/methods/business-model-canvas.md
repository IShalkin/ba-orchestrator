# Business Model Canvas

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.2 Strategy, Goals and Measures`

This card merges 2 descriptions of the method, labelled Variant A–B in every field.

## Problem Solved

**[Variant A]**

Reduces strategic misalignment by mapping on a single page how an enterprise creates, delivers, and captures value across operational, financial, and customer dimensions.

**[Variant B]**

No holistic understanding of how the business creates value

## Uncertainty Reduced

**[Variant B]**

Fragmented elements of a new product's business logic

## BA Lifecycle Phase

**[Variant A]**

Discovery / analysis / strategy / change management

**[Variant B]**

Strategy analysis, definition

## Input Artifacts

**[Variant A]**

Organizational strategy, market analysis, current state description, value proposition ideas.

**[Variant B]**

Enterprise goals, market hypotheses, customer needs

## Output Artifacts

**[Variant A]**

Business Model Canvas (9 building blocks), diagnostic assessment, target business model blueprint.

**[Variant B]**

Result: completed canvas of the 9 business model blocks. Artifacts: Business Model Canvas diagram

## Participants & Roles

**[Variant A]**

Sponsor, Executive/Business Owner, BA, Product Manager, Domain SME.

**[Variant B]**

Sponsor, BA, Product Manager, SME

## Prerequisites

**[Variant A]**

High-level understanding of the enterprise's current or proposed offering and customer base.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

**[Variant A]**

Define the 9 building blocks: Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partnerships, and Cost Structure.
2. Map the current state ('as-is') canvas to diagnose resource allocation and value flow.
3. Identify pain points, gaps, or strategic opportunities across the building blocks.
4. Design future state ('to-be') canvas options.
5. Evaluate inter-dependencies (e.g., how key activities support the value proposition).
6. Use the canvas to guide project portfolio mapping and change strategy.

**[Variant B]**

1. Define the customer segments and value propositions.
2. Describe the channels and relationships.
3. Assess the key resources, partners and processes.
4. Calculate revenues and costs.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

**[Variant A]**

"What value do we deliver to the customer?", "Which customer segments are we serving?", "What are our key revenue streams and cost drivers?", "What key activities drive value creation?"

**[Variant B]**

'Who are our key customers?', 'What value do we deliver to them?'

## Minimal Realistic Example

**[Variant A]**

A software startup drafts a canvas identifying 'Small Businesses' as Customer Segment, 'Cloud Accounting' as Value Proposition, 'Subscription' as Revenue Stream, and 'AWS Infrastructure' as Key Resource.

**[Variant B]**

Describing the business model of a coffee-bean subscription service

## Quality Criteria & Verification

**[Variant A]**

All 9 building blocks are completed and logically connected; clear alignment between Key Activities/Resources and Value Propositions.

**[Variant B]**

Internal consistency of the revenue and cost blocks

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

**[Variant A]**

Treating the canvas as a complete strategy tool while ignoring broader environmental factors; focusing solely on product features instead of holistic value creation.

**[Variant B]**

Superficial filling-in of the blocks without validating hypotheses

## When NOT to Use

**[Variant A]**

For micro-level technical tasks or isolated software feature specs with no impact on the overall business model.

**[Variant B]**

Internal technical refactoring of systems

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

**[Variant A]**

Complemented by SWOT Analysis, Balanced Scorecard, Value Stream Mapping, and Business Capability Analysis.

**[Variant B]**

Value Proposition Canvas, SWOT

## Supported Decisions

**[Variant A]**

Strategic pivot decisions, target operating model selection, product-market fit evaluation.

**[Variant B]**

Scope, business case, architecture

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

**[Variant A]**

AI agents can auto-populate a draft canvas for competitors or new product concepts by analyzing market data and filing reports, providing an instant baseline for human review.

**[Variant B]**

Interactive testing of the blocks' viability with generative AI
