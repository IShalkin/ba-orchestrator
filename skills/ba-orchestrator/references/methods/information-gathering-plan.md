# Information-Gathering Plan

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.5 Interviews, Questionnaires and Individual Techniques`

**Also described as:** Information-Gathering Plan (IGP)

## Problem Solved

Prevents disorganized, haphazard elicitation, eliminates wasted stakeholder meetings, and ensures complete coverage of all required problem domain topics.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Elicitation / Planning

## Input Artifacts

Confirmed real problem statement, initial product scope, stakeholder list.

## Output Artifacts

Information-Gathering Plan (a dynamic matrix mapping required information, sources, methods, and logical sequence).

## Participants & Roles

Business Analyst (author/investigator), SMEs, Process Workers, Business Managers.

## Prerequisites

High-level understanding of the problem domain boundaries.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Column 1 (What Information): Brainstorm all questions and topics needed to understand the problem domain and solution.
2. Filter the topics by asking 'Why will this information help?' and 'What will I learn?'.
3. Column 2 (Source): Identify the specific hard-copy document, process worker role, or system for each topic.
4. Column 3 (Method): Select the appropriate elicitation technique (e.g., read, interview, observe, JAD, prototype).
5. Column 4 (Sequence): Establish a logical top-down sequence for acquiring the information.
6. Execute elicitation following the plan, crossing off topics as information is confirmed, and adding new questions as analysis reveals gaps.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

• What information do I need to understand this process?
• Why will this specific information help solve the problem?
• Where is this information located or who holds it?
• What is the most effective method to capture this information?
• In what order must these topics be investigated?

## Minimal Realistic Example

An analyst builds a 4-column matrix on a whiteboard listing topics ('Layout of vendor tables', 'Voucher entry workflow'), maps sources ('Data dictionary', 'Clerk Charley'), selects methods ('Read', 'Observe'), and orders them 1 to 4. As topics are answered, they are struck through until the board is clear.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

The plan is complete when all required domain topics are mapped to valid sources and methods, and is successfully executed when all plan items are crossed off through validated answers.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Scheduling interviews based on stakeholder calendar availability rather than logical information dependency sequence; gathering requirements without specifying the required information upfront.

## When NOT to Use

Emergency bug fixes where the defect is already fully isolated and documented.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Elicitation Plan, Communication Management Plan, Interview Guides.

## Supported Decisions

Elicitation scheduling, discovery effort estimation, stakeholder engagement strategy.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

An AI agent can maintain a dynamic IGP knowledge graph, automatically identifying missing information nodes, suggesting optimal domain sources, and updating sequence priority as new text inputs are ingested.
