# Boston Box

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.1 External Environment and Market Analysis`

## Problem Solved

Evaluates product and service portfolios based on market share and market growth, reducing bad investment decisions in non-performing assets.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Analysis / Prioritisation

## Input Artifacts

Product catalog, market share metrics, industry market growth trends, sales figures.

## Output Artifacts

2x2 Matrix mapping products into Star, Cash Cow, Wild Cat/Problem Child, and Dog quadrants.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Product Manager, Marketing Manager, Business Owner, Sponsor.

## Prerequisites

Reliable market share and industry growth metrics for all evaluated offerings.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

List all products/services in the portfolio.
2. Determine market growth rate for each product's industry.
3. Determine relative market share for each product.
4. Map products onto the 2x2 grid (Star, Cash Cow, Wild Cat, Dog).
5. Formulate product investment/divestment strategies.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Which products operate in high-growth markets? Which products dominate their market share? Which offerings drain capital without growth prospects?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A software vendor maps its legacy desktop software to 'Cash Cow' (funding new web apps) and an underperforming legacy utility to 'Dog' (flagged for retirement).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Accurate categorization reflecting objective market data rather than internal executive fondness for legacy products.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Forcing products that sit on quadrant boundaries into rigid categories; misjudging low market share as failure when it is caused by market timing.

## When NOT to Use

For single-product startups or non-commercial internal IT systems without market competition.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

BCG Matrix, Ansoff's Matrix, Value Proposition Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Product portfolio investment, divestment, resource allocation, release priorities.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI analytics automatically categorize product lines by analyzing real-time sales velocity against industry market growth APIs.
