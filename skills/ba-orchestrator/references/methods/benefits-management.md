# Benefits Management

`T8 Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation › 8.3 Benefits and Outcome Evaluation`

**Also described as:** Benefits management

## Problem Solved

Manages a project continuously against a formal Benefits Plan and Benefits Dependency Framework, maximizing the probability that business benefits are actually achieved.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Manage Change / Benefits Management

## Input Artifacts

Business Case, Objectives, Benefit descriptions, Enabling and Business changes list.

## Output Artifacts

Benefits Dependency Framework / Map, Benefits Plan (Vision, Maps, Timeline Bar Chart, Metrics, Financials, Accountabilities/Owners, Review tracking).

## Participants & Roles

BA, Project Sponsor, Change Manager, Benefit Owners (Line Managers).

## Prerequisites

Identification of expected business benefits and business case approval.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify overall business Objectives.
2. Identify required Benefits.
3. Identify necessary Business Changes.
4. Identify required Enabling Changes.
5. Construct Benefits Dependency Framework/Map.
6. Map against timeline bar chart.
7. Assign named Benefit Owners.
8. Manage project against Benefits Plan, reviewing at stage gates.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What core business objective are we serving? What specific benefits achieve this objective? What business and enabling changes are required? Who is the named Benefit Owner?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Creating a Benefits Map for customer support: Enabling changes (tech spec, training) -> Business changes (adopt new service) -> Benefits (online support, faster response) -> Objective (Improved Customer Satisfaction).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Complete logical chain from Enabling Changes -> Business Changes -> Benefits -> Objectives; named Benefit Owners with authority assigned to every benefit.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Focusing purely on IT system delivery while neglecting necessary business/enabling changes; failing to assign benefit owners; treating benefits as a static business case appendix.

## When NOT to Use

In small, non-investment experimental research spikes where benefits cannot be structured.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Benefits Dependency Framework, Benefits Realisation, Cost-Benefit Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Project stage-gate approval, scope change impact evaluation, benefit owner assignment.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Benefits tracking AI engines monitor project execution milestones and operational metrics, dynamically updating Benefits Dependency Maps and alerting owners to risks.
