# Cost-Benefit Analysis

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.5 Business Case`

**Also described as:** Cost/Benefit Analysis; Cost–benefit analysis

This card merges 2 descriptions of the method, labelled Variant A–B in every field.

## Problem Solved

**[Variant A]**

Identifies, quantifies, and contrasts the expected costs and benefits of a proposed solution option across Tangible/Intangible and Immediate/Longer-term categories.

**[Variant B]**

Evaluates project financial viability and compares hard monetary returns against soft quality factors to justify IT investments.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

**[Variant A]**

Evaluate Options / Prepare Business Case

**[Variant B]**

elicitation

## Input Artifacts

**[Variant A]**

Option descriptions, vendor quotes, HR day rates, market research, operational metrics.

**[Variant B]**

Business goals, vendor price quotes, operational cost estimates.

## Output Artifacts

**[Variant A]**

Cost-Benefit Statement categorized by Tangible Immediate/Longer-term Costs and Tangible/Intangible Immediate/Longer-term Benefits.

**[Variant B]**

Cost/Benefit Table (Hard Benefits, Hard Costs, Net Present Value, Payback Period, Soft Factors score).

## Participants & Roles

**[Variant A]**

BA, Sponsor, Finance Manager, Business User Leads, Solution Vendors.

**[Variant B]**

BA, sponsor, financial manager, IT manager.

## Prerequisites

**[Variant A]**

Selected option definition and access to organizational financial/resource cost data.

**[Variant B]**

Estimates of project costs and quantifiable business benefits.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

**[Variant A]**

Identify one-off initial tangible costs (Hardware, software, development, training, data cleanup).
2. Identify ongoing tangible costs (Maintenance, support, salaries).
3. Identify intangible costs (Recruitment, short-term productivity loss).
4. Identify tangible benefits (Staff savings, reduced effort, avoided costs).
5. Identify intangible benefits (Job satisfaction, customer satisfaction, brand image).
6. Quantify tangible items in financial terms.

**[Variant B]**

Identify hard benefits ($/year saved or gained).
2. Identify hard costs (supplier price, hardware, training, operational maintenance).
3. Discount future cash flows to calculate Net Present Value (NPV), Payback Period, and ROI.
4. Identify soft benefit factors (e.g., IT flexibility, stress reduction) and rate on a 0–5 scale.
5. Present combined hard/soft analysis to executive management.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

**[Variant A]**

What are the one-off startup costs? What are the ongoing maintenance costs? What tangible cost savings result? What intangible benefits will be realized? Can this benefit be credibly monetized in advance?

**[Variant B]**

How much money do we lose annually from these errors? What is the expected payback period for this investment?

## Minimal Realistic Example

**[Variant A]**

Quantifying a new system: One-off costs (£350k hardware/software, £60k ongoing support) vs Tangible benefits (£80k staff savings/yr, £100k increased sales/yr) and Intangible benefit (Improved brand image).

**[Variant B]**

Hard Benefits ( $9M) minus Hard Costs ($ 1.7M) yielding NPV = $7.3M and payback in 2.5 years, alongside Soft Factor gains in stress reduction.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

**[Variant A]**

Tangible claims must be backed by credible, pre-verifiable evidence; clear distinction between features and actual benefits.

**[Variant B]**

Financial audit review, ROI/payback threshold check.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

**[Variant A]**

Claiming intangible benefits as tangible without credible pre-quantification; falling for the 'FTE fallacy' (aggregating fractional staff time savings that cannot actually be realized as real cash savings); double-counting accommodation costs.

**[Variant B]**

Manipulating soft factors artificially to force a positive net value for a non-viable project.

## When NOT to Use

**[Variant A]**

When evaluating mandatory regulatory options where execution is legally compulsory regardless of cost-benefit ratios.

**[Variant B]**

Mandatory legal/regulatory compliance projects where the system must be built regardless of cost.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

**[Variant A]**

CBA, BCA, Benefits Categorisation, Investment Appraisal.

**[Variant B]**

Goal-Domain Tracing, QFD.

## Supported Decisions

**[Variant A]**

Business case financial justification, project authorization, option comparison.

**[Variant B]**

Project charter approval, go/no-go investment decisions, vendor selection.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

**[Variant A]**

Financial AI tools analyze historical project expenditure and operational metrics to predict cost-benefit figures for new option proposals automatically.

**[Variant B]**

Run multi-variable NPV, payback, and sensitivity analyses automatically based on estimated cost parameters using financial AI models.
