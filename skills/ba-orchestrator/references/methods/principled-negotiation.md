# Principled Negotiation

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.3 Engagement, Conflict and Negotiation`

**Also described as:** Principled negotiation

## Problem Solved

Resolves stakeholder disagreements by focusing on interests rather than positions, separating people from the problem, and using objective criteria, reducing destructive positional bargaining.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Stakeholder Management

## Input Artifacts

Stakeholder positions, CATWOE outputs, requirement trade-offs.

## Output Artifacts

Negotiated Agreement, defined BATNA (Best Alternative to a Negotiated Agreement) and warning levels.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Negotiator/Facilitator), Disagreeing Stakeholders.

## Prerequisites

A disagreement over requirements, scope, or project choices.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Separate the people from the problem (manage emotions/perceptions).
2. Focus on underlying interests rather than stated positions (ask 'Why?').
3. Brainstorm a variety of creative options for mutual gain.
4. Establish objective evaluation criteria.
5. Define BATNA and warning levels for all parties.
6. Agree on option satisfying criteria.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Why is that position important to you? What underlying interest does that satisfy? What objective benchmark can we use to evaluate options? What is your BATNA if we don't agree?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Instead of arguing over a demand for 'instant phone repairs' (position), the BA asks 'Why?', uncovers the interest ('preventing missed client calls'), and agrees on 'call-forwarding during repairs'.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Agreement satisfies the true underlying interests of all parties based on objective criteria; clear pre-defined BATNAs.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Entrenching in positional arguments; making personal attacks; accepting bad deals below the pre-set BATNA warning level.

## When NOT to Use

When dealing with strict non-negotiable statutory legal mandates.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

BATNA, Thomas-Kilmann Instrument, CATWOE.

## Supported Decisions

Requirements trade-offs, scope approval, vendor contract terms, consensus building.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI mediation bots analyze conflicting interests, evaluate objective benchmarks, and generate win-win candidate solution options automatically.
