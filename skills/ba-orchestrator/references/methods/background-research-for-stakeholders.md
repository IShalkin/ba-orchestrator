# Background Research for Stakeholders

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.1 Stakeholder Identification`

**Also described as:** Background research

## Problem Solved

Uncovers hidden, external, or non-obvious stakeholders and organizational context by systematically analyzing existing project and organizational documentation.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Identification

## Input Artifacts

Project Initiation Document (PID), feasibility reports, terms of reference, org charts, organization diagrams.

## Output Artifacts

Expanded stakeholder list, organizational structure maps.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Researcher).

## Prerequisites

Access to relevant organizational, pre-project, and historical project documentation.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Gather pre-project documents (Feasibility studies, Terms of Reference, PIDs).
2. Review Organization Charts and Organization Diagrams.
3. Identify departments, external groups, or managers mentioned.
4. Add identified individuals and groups to the stakeholder list.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What previous studies examined this area? Which external suppliers or customer groups are mapped in organization diagrams? Which departments appear on org charts as linked to this process?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA reads a 2-year-old feasibility study, discovering that the legal compliance team and an external auditing partner were listed as key impacted parties.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Thorough extraction of all named internal and external groups from available project documentation.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Failing to look for pre-project documents; ignoring org chart units that appear peripherally related.

## When NOT to Use

When no prior documentation exists for a completely novel greenfield startup venture.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Stakeholder Nomination, Stakeholder Wheel, Organization Diagram.

## Supported Decisions

Stakeholder engagement scope, risk analysis, project boundary setting.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Document-mining AI agents ingest repositories of legacy project files and org charts to output a consolidated list of historical stakeholders.
