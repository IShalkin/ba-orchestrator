# Business Impact Analysis

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.5 Business Case`

**Also described as:** Impact analysis

## Problem Solved

Identifies and presents non-cost organizational, cultural, and procedural impacts of a proposed change, ensuring decision-makers have a complete picture beyond financial numbers.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Evaluate Options / Prepare Business Case

## Input Artifacts

Selected solution option description, process models, organization charts, POPIT assessment.

## Output Artifacts

Impact Analysis Report detailing non-cost impacts (Org structure, Interdepartmental relations, Working practices, Management style, Recruitment, Promotion, Customer focus, Supplier relations).

## Participants & Roles

BA, Change Manager, HR Lead, Operations Leads, Sponsor.

## Prerequisites

Definition of the recommended solution option.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Analyze impact on Organizational Structure and interdepartmental relations.
2. Analyze impact on Working Practices and union agreements.
3. Analyze impact on Management Style (e.g. shift to coaching).
4. Analyze impact on Recruitment, Promotion criteria, and Skills.
5. Analyze impact on Customer Focus and Supplier relations.
6. Formulate mitigation actions for negative impacts.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

How does this option change daily working practices? Does it require shifting management style from command-and-control to coaching? How does it affect supplier or union relationships?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Identifying that flattening management layers (Option impact) requires supervisors to shift from 'command-and-control' to a 'coaching' style, requiring targeted leadership development.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Holistic coverage of soft organizational and human impacts alongside hard structural changes; actionable mitigation proposals.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Omitting non-cost cultural/political impacts from the business case, leading to executive surprise and project rejection during rollout.

## When NOT to Use

For minor isolated technical bug fixes with zero process or human footprint.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

POPIT Model, McKinsey 7-S, Leavitt's Diamond, Force-Field Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Business case approval, target operating model design, change management intervention planning.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Impact prediction AI scans proposed solution feature lists against historical HR and process data to highlight probable cultural and working practice friction areas.
