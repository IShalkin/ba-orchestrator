# Kotter's 8 Steps

`T8 Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation › 8.1 Organisational Change`

**Also described as:** Kotter’s approach to change

## Problem Solved

Provides a comprehensive 8-step framework (Urgency, Coalition, Vision, Communicate, Remove obstacles, Short-term wins, Build, Anchor) for leading successful organizational change.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Manage Change / People Change / Organisational Change

## Input Artifacts

Business Case, Strategy definition, Stakeholder analysis, POPIT assessment.

## Output Artifacts

8-Step Change Execution Roadmap and Communication Plan.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Executive Sponsor, Guiding Coalition Leads, Change Manager.

## Prerequisites

High-level authorization for major business change.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Create sense of Urgency (get ~75% manager buy-in).
2. Form a powerful Guiding Coalition.
3. Create Vision and Strategy.
4. Communicate Vision broadly.
5. Remove obstacles (align structures, systems, rewards).
6. Create Short-Term Wins.
7. Build on change (don't declare victory early).
8. Anchor changes in Corporate Culture.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Have we created a true sense of urgency? Who forms our guiding coalition? What is our concise vision strap line? What short-term win can we deliver first?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Implementing automated airport border control:
1. Build urgency around queues -> 2. Form coalition (ministers, border leads) -> 3. Define vision 'Speed & Security' -> 4. Communicate -> 5. Align staff -> 6. Pilot short-term win -> 7. Roll out -> 8. Anchor in culture.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Sequential execution of all 8 steps; >75% management buy-in established in Step 1; early short-term wins achieved and celebrated.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Declaring victory after the first short-term win; skipping Step 1 (Urgency) and rushing straight to execution; lacking a powerful guiding coalition.

## When NOT to Use

For small, routine micro-tasks or isolated technical maintenance with no organizational reach.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Kurt Lewin's Change Model, POPIT Model, Cultural Web, SARAH Model.

## Supported Decisions

Change roadmap planning, stakeholder coalition assembly, change communication execution.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Change management platforms track progress metrics across all 8 Kotter steps, alerting leaders if steps are skipped or short-term wins are lacking.
