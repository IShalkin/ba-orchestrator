# Feasibility Assessment and Business Case Development

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.5 Business Case`

## Problem Solved

Reduces financial, technical, and business uncertainty before committing resources to a change initiative.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Analysis / Prioritisation

## Input Artifacts

Problem statements, option descriptions, cost and benefit estimates, risk logs, strategic context.

## Output Artifacts

Business Case, Feasibility Report, CARDI/RAID log, Investment Appraisal (NPV, IRR, Payback), Benefits Plan.

## Participants & Roles

Sponsor, Business Owner, BA, Finance SME, Solution Architect, Project Manager.

## Prerequisites

Clear definition of the business problem, identified options for solution, initial cost and benefit data.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Generate and describe options to resolve the problem.
2. Remove unviable options.
3. Identify and analyse impacts and risks for each option.
4. Identify and quantify costs and benefits.
5. Evaluate financial, technical, and business feasibility.
6. Evaluate strategic alignment (e.g., VMOST, POPIT).
7. Produce the business case and present recommendations.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Is this solution technically feasible? Can we afford it? What is the ROI/NPV? Does it fit our culture and strategy? What are the risks of doing nothing?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Evaluating three options for an event registration system (basic manual upgrade, COTS package, bespoke build), proving through NPV that the COTS package offers the best risk-adjusted return.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Passes 'decision gates', demonstrates positive NPV/IRR, satisfies Force-Field analysis, clear alignment with corporate strategy.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Over-promising intangible benefits, underestimating transition costs, presenting a single preferred solution without evaluating alternatives (including 'do nothing').

## When NOT to Use

When changes are minor, low-risk, or strictly mandated by legal/compliance requirements with no alternative implementation routes.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Financial models (NPV, Payback, IRR), PESTLE, Force-Field Analysis, POPIT, CARDI/RAID logs.

## Supported Decisions

Scope, financial commitment, project go/no-go, option selection.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI agents can run probabilistic financial simulations (e.g., Monte Carlo) on costs/benefits and perform real-time market/vendor analysis for COTS feasibility.
