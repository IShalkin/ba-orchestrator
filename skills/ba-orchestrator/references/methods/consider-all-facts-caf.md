# Consider-All-Facts (CAF)

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.3 Engagement, Conflict and Negotiation`

## Problem Solved

Gathers all influencing factors surrounding a requirement conflict to prevent missing hidden drivers during negotiation.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Validation / Negotiation

## Input Artifacts

Conflict description, stakeholder goals, domain constraints

## Output Artifacts

Comprehensive list of prioritized conflict factors

## Participants & Roles

Requirements Engineer / Conflict Manager, Disputing Stakeholders

## Prerequisites

Identified requirement conflict between parties.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify the core conflict between stakeholders.
2. Brainstorm all possible factors influencing or affected by the conflict (costs, technical limits, user comfort, schedule).
3. Document all facts without immediate filtering.
4. Prioritize the importance of each factor.
5. Feed factors into decision techniques like PMI or Decision Matrix.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"What underlying factors are influencing this disagreement?", "Which external or internal constraints impact this decision?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

To resolve a conflict over automatic map updating frequency, the analyst lists data bandwidth costs, battery consumption, driver safety, and server capacity.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Thoroughness of identified factors; agreement by both conflicting parties that all relevant facts are listed.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Overlooking subtle organizational or political influences on the conflict.

## When NOT to Use

When time constraints require an immediate executive decision (overruling).

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Plus-Minus-Interesting (PMI), Decision Matrix

## Supported Decisions

Conflict analysis, trade-off evaluation.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Automated retrieval of domain constraints, compliance rules, and technical logs to present an objective fact sheet to disputing parties.
