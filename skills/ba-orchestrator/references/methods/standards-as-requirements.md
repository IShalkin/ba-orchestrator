# Standards as Requirements

`T5 Requirements Specification › 5.5 Non-Functional Requirements`

## Problem Solved

Transfers detailed regulatory, platform, or interface specifications to external standard documents, avoiding redundant specification writing.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

specification

## Input Artifacts

Industry regulations, corporate style guides, technical interface manuals.

## Output Artifacts

Textual requirement statements referencing external standards.

## Participants & Roles

BA, compliance officer, legal advisor, developer.

## Prerequisites

Identification of applicable legal, regulatory, or technical standards.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify applicable external domain or technical standards.
2. Verify the relevance and version of the standard.
3. Draft requirement statements referencing the specific standard and release.
4. Define compliance and certification responsibilities (e.g., supplier must obtain certificate).
5. Specify ongoing maintenance obligations for evolving standards.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Which union agreements or accounting laws apply? Which specific version of the platform style guide must be enforced?

## Minimal Realistic Example

R4: The product shall follow good accounting practice. The supplier shall obtain the necessary certification.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Third-party certification, compliance audit reviews.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Blindly referencing standards without understanding their business goals or practical verification costs.

## When NOT to Use

When custom, project-specific behavior is required that contradicts public standards.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Feature Requirements, Process Descriptions.

## Supported Decisions

Legal compliance, technical architecture alignment, platform selection.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Scan requirement documents for regulatory gaps against pre-loaded compliance standard frameworks using AI agents.
