# Creativity Workshops and Triggers

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.6 Group and Creative Sessions`

**Also described as:** Creativity Workshops & Triggers

## Problem Solved

Prevents teams from settling for pedestrian, incremental, or presumed solutions by systematically generating innovative, high-value candidate ideas.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery

## Input Artifacts

Problem Statement, Project Goals, User Personas, Constraints.

## Output Artifacts

Innovative Candidate Solutions, Brainstorming Logs, Value Propositions, Slogans/Elevator Pitches.

## Participants & Roles

Facilitator (BA), Innovation Team (SMEs, Designers, Developers, Product Owner).

## Prerequisites

Safe, non-judgmental environment; clearly defined business problem statement.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Assemble a diverse, 4–8 person cross-functional team.
2. Establish ground rules: no criticism, no idea killers, suspend constraints.
3. Apply Creative Triggers (Participation, Convenience, Connections, Information, Feeling, Speed, Grandchildren).
4. Apply Lateral Thinking (challenge assumptions, remove constraints, combine existing concepts).
5. Generate a large volume of candidate ideas.
6. Group, combine, and refine candidate ideas into sacrificial candidate prototypes.
7. Evaluate and select promising ideas for further probing.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"What would make this service 10x more convenient?", "How can we involve the customer in doing this work?", "What if we had zero budget / no time limit?", "What solution would your grandchildren use?", "What if we removed specific constraint?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

Applying the 'Convenience' trigger to metro ticketing, asking 'What if commuters didn't need to buy tickets?', leading to the idea of contactless credit card/phone entry at turnstiles.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

High volume and diversity of generated ideas; team feels safe to propose radical ideas; at least 3 distinct candidate solutions created for probing.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Allowing immediate criticism or 'idea killers' during generation; focusing on the first obvious idea; settling for minor incremental tweaks.

## When NOT to Use

Strict regulatory compliance tasks where solutions are 100% legally prescribed with zero room for variation.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Design Sprints, TRIZ, SCAMPER, Lateral Thinking Puzzles, Brainstorming.

## Supported Decisions

Product strategy, innovative feature selection, breakthrough solution architecture.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI ideation tools (e.g., Design Sparks) can be prompted with business problem statements and creative triggers to generate hundreds of unexpected candidate solution concepts for workshop evaluation.
