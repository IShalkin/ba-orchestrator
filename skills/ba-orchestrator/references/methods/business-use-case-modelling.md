# Business Use Case Modelling

`T5 Requirements Specification › 5.2 Use Cases and Scenarios`

**Also described as:** Business use case modelling

## Problem Solved

Models high-level business services provided by an organization/domain to actors, independent of technology or internal execution mechanics, clarifying strategic business scope.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Define Requirements / Requirements Modelling

## Input Artifacts

Organization Diagram, Value Chain, CATWOE outputs, stakeholder list.

## Output Artifacts

Business Use Case Diagram (Actors, Business Use Cases, Associations, optional system boundary) and Business Use Case Descriptions.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Business Architect, Enterprise Architect, Business Owners.

## Prerequisites

High-level understanding of business domain goals and services.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify high-level business services required by stakeholders.
2. Represent services as Business Use Cases (ovals, using standard or striped Podeswa notation).
3. Identify human/external system Business Actors (stick figures).
4. Draw association lines connecting actors to business use cases.
5. Review business services with stakeholders.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What core business services does this organization provide to its clients? Who are the primary actors accessing these services? What business value is delivered?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A Business Use Case Diagram for an HR domain showing actors HR Manager and HR Officer interacting with business use cases 'Recruit staff member', 'Induct staff member', and 'Conduct annual appraisal'.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Models business-level services (what), not IT software functions or physical step procedures; covers all core stakeholder services.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Confusing high-level business use cases with low-level IT system software functions; adding technical screen details.

## When NOT to Use

When analyzing micro-level software coding logic or database schema architecture.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Use Case Modelling, Value Streams, Capability Modelling, Business Activity Modelling.

## Supported Decisions

Business domain scope definition, target operating model design, service delivery channel selection.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Enterprise architecture AI parses corporate strategy documents to draft candidate Business Use Case Diagrams automatically.
