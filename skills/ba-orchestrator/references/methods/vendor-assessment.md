# Vendor Assessment

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.4 Solution Options and Feasibility`

## Problem Solved

Reduces third-party procurement risk by formally evaluating vendor stability, capability, licensing models, and compliance before committing to a commercial solution.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Strategy / analysis / Solution Evaluation

## Input Artifacts

RFI, RFP, RFQ, RFT, vendor proposals, non-functional requirements, SLA expectations.

## Output Artifacts

Vendor assessment report, vendor scorecard, licensing/pricing model comparison, risk evaluation.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Procurement Lead, Sponsor, Technical Architect, Compliance/Legal.

## Prerequisites

Clear business and non-functional requirements for the third-party product/service.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Issue procurement request (RFI/RFP/RFQ) detailing business requirements and SLAs.
2. Evaluate vendor knowledge, domain expertise, and technical capability.
3. Analyze vendor licensing, pricing models, and total cost of ownership.
4. Assess vendor market position, experience, reputation, and financial stability.
5. Evaluate legal terms, IP rights, data protection, and product roadmap.
6. Score vendors against evaluation criteria and make selection recommendation.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"Does the vendor meet our security and regulatory compliance standards?", "What is the vendor's financial stability and market reputation?", "What are the total licensing and customization costs over 5 years?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

A vendor evaluation scorecard rates 3 Cloud CRM vendors across Security (30%), TCO (30%), and Feature Coverage (40%); Vendor B achieves highest score of 88/100.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Objective scoring criteria used; due diligence performed on financial/legal terms; non-functional SLAs evaluated.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Selecting a vendor based purely on low initial price while ignoring ongoing TCO and vendor stability risks; subjective biased scoring.

## When NOT to Use

When building an in-house custom solution with no third-party software, hardware, or consulting vendor involvement.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Complemented by Decision Analysis, Acceptance & Evaluation Criteria, Financial Analysis, and RFPs.

## Supported Decisions

Vendor selection, commercial software procurement, outsourcing contracts, SLA approval.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI agents can ingest hundreds of pages of vendor RFP responses to auto-extract pricing models, SLA terms, and compliance matrices for analyst review.
