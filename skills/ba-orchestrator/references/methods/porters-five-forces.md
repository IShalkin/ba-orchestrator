# Porter's Five Forces

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.1 External Environment and Market Analysis`

**Also described as:** Porter's Five Forces framework

## Problem Solved

Determines the competitive intensity and profitability of an industry or market domain, reducing strategic blind spots regarding industry competitive pressures.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Strategy Analysis

## Input Artifacts

Industry data, market research reports, product/service portfolio descriptions.

## Output Artifacts

Industry pressure assessment, competitive position summary, SWOT opportunities and threats.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Product Manager, Business Owner, Senior Executives, Strategy Analysts.

## Prerequisites

Clear definition of the specific industry or market domain being evaluated.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Define the target industry/domain boundaries.
2. Assess threat of new entrants and barriers to entry.
3. Assess bargaining power of buyers.
4. Assess bargaining power of suppliers.
5. Assess threat of substitute products/services.
6. Evaluate rivalry among existing industry competitors.
7. Synthesize competitive pressures into strategic threats/opportunities.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What is the level of competition? How easy is it for new players to enter? How much choice do buyers have? Do suppliers hold power over pricing? What alternative substitutes fulfill the same core need?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A luxury handbag retailer defines its industry as 'luxury giftware' instead of 'leather bags', identifying fine jewelry and perfume as key competitive substitutes.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Comprehensive coverage of all five forces based on factual industry data rather than subjective organizational assumptions.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Defining the industry too narrowly or broadly; missing non-obvious substitute products; ignoring high-capital potential entrants such as mega-supermarkets.

## When NOT to Use

In non-competitive internal process optimization or when analyzing internal team dynamics/operations.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

PESTLE analysis, SWOT analysis, Value Chain Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Market entry/exit, competitive strategy, pricing strategy, business model selection.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Agentic scrapers monitor market entry, price changes, supplier consolidation, and consumer shift trends to populate force vectors automatically.
