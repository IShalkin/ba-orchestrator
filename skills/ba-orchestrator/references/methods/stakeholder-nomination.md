# Stakeholder Nomination

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.1 Stakeholder Identification`

**Also described as:** Stakeholder nomination

## Problem Solved

Identifies initial project stakeholders through direct recommendation from leaders and interviewees, reducing missed key project contacts.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Identification

## Input Artifacts

Sponsor list, preliminary project brief.

## Output Artifacts

Initial stakeholder nomination list.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Project Sponsor, Senior Managers.

## Prerequisites

Access to project sponsor or commissioning manager.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Interview Project Sponsor to identify key business leads.
2. Interview named leads and ask them to nominate key team members.
3. Cascade nominations downward through organizational levels.
4. Compile master nominated stakeholder list.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Who else should I speak to about this? Who has detailed operational knowledge of this process? Who will be impacted by changes here?

## Minimal Realistic Example

The BA interviews the Project Sponsor, who nominates the Operations Manager, who in turn nominates three senior claims handling clerks.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Coverage of key operational leads and decision-makers across the affected business areas.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Relying solely on nominated stakeholders, resulting in bias toward people who agree with the sponsor while missing critical or opposing perspectives.

## When NOT to Use

As the sole method for stakeholder identification without combining with structured tools like the Stakeholder Wheel.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Stakeholder Wheel, Background Research, Organization Diagram.

## Supported Decisions

Interview planning, workshop participant selection, stakeholder engagement strategy.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Organizational chart parsing tools suggest cascading lists of candidate stakeholders based on initial manager nominations.
