# Business Case

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.5 Business Case`

**Also described as:** Business Cases; Business case report creation; Vision Document & Business Case Development

This card merges 4 descriptions of the method, labelled Variant A–D in every field.

## Problem Solved

**[Variant A]**

Reduces financial and strategic uncertainty by providing a formal justification for investing resources in a proposed solution compared to alternative options or doing nothing.

**[Variant B]**

Assembles all analytical findings, option evaluations, cost-benefit models, and impact/risk assessments into a structured, convincing written business report for executive decision-makers.

**[Variant C]**

Reduces financial uncertainty, politically motivated project selection, and unclear business value/feasibility.

**[Variant D]**

No justification of whether an investment is worthwhile

## Uncertainty Reduced

**[Variant D]**

Uncertainty about payback and financial value

## BA Lifecycle Phase

**[Variant A]**

Discovery / analysis / prioritisation / change management

**[Variant B]**

Evaluate Options / Present Business Case

**[Variant C]**

Discovery / prioritisation

**[Variant D]**

Strategy analysis, solution evaluation

## Input Artifacts

**[Variant A]**

Business need, current state description, solution options, cost-benefit estimates, risk analysis.

**[Variant B]**

Gap Analysis, Option evaluations, Cost-Benefit Analysis, Investment Appraisal, Impact/Risk analysis outputs.

**[Variant C]**

Problem statements, business process models, market research, initial cost estimates.

**[Variant D]**

Cost estimates, projected benefits, risks

## Output Artifacts

**[Variant A]**

Business Case document/presentation (containing need assessment, desired outcomes, alternative analysis, and recommended solution).

**[Variant B]**

Written Business Case Report (Introduction, Management Summary, Background, Options, Benefits & Costs, Investment Appraisal, Impacts, Risks, Recommendations, Appendices).

**[Variant C]**

Vision Document, Business Case (cash flow analysis, ROI).

**[Variant D]**

Result: business case document with ROI, NPV and payback period. Artifacts: business case document, financial model

## Participants & Roles

**[Variant A]**

Sponsor, Business Owner, BA, Project Manager, Financial Analyst, Domain SME.

**[Variant B]**

BA (Author), Sponsor (Reviewer/Owner), Decision-Makers (Audience).

**[Variant C]**

Sponsor, Business Owner/Executive Manager, BA/System Analyst, Project Manager.

**[Variant D]**

Sponsor, BA, Financial Analyst, Business Owner

## Prerequisites

**[Variant A]**

Clearly defined business problem/opportunity and preliminary estimation of costs/benefits.

**[Variant B]**

Completion of option evaluation, cost-benefit, and risk analysis.

**[Variant C]**

Concept initialization and initial business opportunity identification.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

**[Variant A]**

Define the core business need and align it with strategic objectives.
2. Detail desired measurable outcomes independent of the specific solution.
3. Identify and assess solution alternatives (including scope, feasibility, risks, and financial ROI/NPV).
4. Evaluate the 'Do Nothing' option as a baseline.
5. Select and justify the recommended solution with cost and timeline estimates.
6. Present to decision makers for approval and funding.

**[Variant B]**

Write Background and Introduction.
2. Document Options considered and rejection reasons.
3. Document Benefits and Costs (benefits first).
4. Include Investment Appraisal summary.
5. Detail Impacts and Risks with mitigations.
6. State clear Recommendations and required decision.
7. Move detailed calculations to Appendices.
8. Write concise 3-paragraph Management Summary last.
9. Proofread thoroughly.

**[Variant C]**

Qualify and define the core problem to be solved.
2. Identify customers, stakeholders, and market positioning.
3. Quantify expected tangible and intangible benefits.
4. Estimate project costs (personnel, capital, time).
5. Perform risk analysis and calculate return on investment (e.g., cash flow method).
6. Present to stakeholders for go/no-go decision.

**[Variant D]**

1. Describe the business need.
2. Estimate the costs (TCO).
3. Estimate the benefits.
4. Financial analysis (NPV, ROI).
5. Defend it before the sponsor.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

**[Variant A]**

"What business problem are we solving?", "What are the quantifiable financial and non-financial benefits?", "What is the cost and risk of doing nothing?", "What is the ROI and payback period?"

**[Variant B]**

What is the core recommendation? What decision is required from executive management? Are the financial details accessible in appendices? Is the Executive Summary concise and focused?

**[Variant C]**

What problem are we solving? If completed, what impact will this have on profitability or performance? What are the risks?

**[Variant D]**

'What is the financial return on the invested capital?', 'Which risks could derail the payback?'

## Minimal Realistic Example

**[Variant A]**

A business case demonstrates that spending $200k on automated invoice processing will save $100k/year in labor, breaking even in 2 years with a net positive NPV.

**[Variant B]**

A BA drafts a 10-page Business Case Report with a 1-page Management Summary recommending option 1 (buy package), attaching 40 pages of detailed DCF tables in appendices.

**[Variant C]**

Loan system projected to cost $2.7M over 6 quarters but yield $1.8M quarterly after launch by reducing turnaround time from 2 weeks to 48 hours.

**[Variant D]**

Calculating the 2-year payback of an automated warehouse

## Quality Criteria & Verification

**[Variant A]**

Clear linkage to strategy; realistic and verifiable financial assumptions; objective assessment of risks and alternatives.

**[Variant B]**

Concise, active-voice writing style; clear 3-paragraph Management Summary; explicit recommendation and requested decision; zero typos.

**[Variant C]**

Executive sign-off, stakeholder walkthrough, financial break-even review.

**[Variant D]**

Financial audit, sign-off by the CFO

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

**[Variant A]**

Author bias exaggerating benefits and underestimating costs; failing to update the business case after funding is approved.

**[Variant B]**

Writing an overly long, verbose report without a clear Executive Summary; burying key recommendations in dense text; passive academic writing style ('It is recommended that...'); draft errors.

**[Variant C]**

Proceeding without quantifiable business value; underestimating software costs by omitting risk multipliers.

**[Variant D]**

Overstated benefits and understated development and support costs

## When NOT to Use

**[Variant A]**

For small, mandatory low-cost changes (e.g., urgent regulatory patch) where the cost of writing the business case exceeds the change cost.

**[Variant B]**

When presenting micro-level operational tweaks where a simple verbal briefing or email request is sufficient.

**[Variant C]**

Small-scale, low-risk, internal ad-hoc bug fixes or minor enhancements with guaranteed zero financial exposure.

**[Variant D]**

Mandatory regulatory (compliance) requirements where there is no choice

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

**[Variant A]**

Complemented by Financial Analysis, Decision Analysis, Feasibility Study, and SWOT Analysis.

**[Variant B]**

Business Case Presentation, 4As Communication Model.

**[Variant C]**

Feasibility Study, Statement of Work (SOW), Concept of Operations (ConOps).

**[Variant D]**

Cost-Benefit Analysis

## Supported Decisions

**[Variant A]**

Project funding/approval, solution alternative selection, go/no-go change strategy decisions.

**[Variant B]**

Project authorization, capital expenditure approval, strategic option selection.

**[Variant C]**

Scope, project approval (go/no-go), release budget, priority.

**[Variant D]**

Scope, priority, release, business case

**[Variant B]**

GenAI report-authoring tools compile underlying analytical models into a standardized, beautifully formatted Business Case Report draft.

**[Variant C]**

Not specified in source.
