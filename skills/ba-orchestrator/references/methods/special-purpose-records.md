# Special-Purpose Records

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.4 Domain, Document and Data Research`

**Also described as:** Special-purpose records

## Problem Solved

Gathers quantitative data on activity volumes and time spent directly from business users, reducing analyst observation overhead.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Elicitation

## Input Artifacts

Special-purpose record sheet, tallies template, activity categories list.

## Output Artifacts

Completed tally sheets, volume/time logs, aggregated task distribution data.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Form Designer), Business Users (Record Keepers).

## Prerequisites

Briefing session with users to explain form completion and secure buy-in.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify activity categories via preliminary interviews.
2. Design an easy-to-use tally/timesheet form.
3. Brief participating staff on how and when to record entries.
4. Staff keep tallies during their working week.
5. Collect forms and aggregate daily/weekly data.
6. Analyze volume and time trends.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

How many times did issue X occur today? How much time was spent dealing with complaint category Y?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Complaints handling staff use a daily 5-bar gate tally sheet for a week to record complaint volumes across 6 categories, revealing non-delivery as the top issue.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Consistent, real-time completion by staff throughout the day rather than end-of-day guesswork.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Designing overly complex forms with too many categories; user guesswork caused by end-of-day completion; staff inflating numbers to justify resources.

## When NOT to Use

When staff are uncooperative or when high-precision objective timing is required.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Sampling, Document Analysis, Activity Sampling.

## Supported Decisions

Process prioritization, workload distribution, business case savings estimation.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Digital micro-logging widgets embedded in user dashboards allow 1-click activity tagging, feeding real-time analytics engines.
