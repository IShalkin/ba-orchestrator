# Force-Field Analysis

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.4 Solution Options and Feasibility`

**Also described as:** Force-field analysis

## Problem Solved

Identifies driving forces (+ positive) and restraining forces (- negative) acting upon a proposed change option, guiding strategies to strengthen drivers and reduce resistance.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Evaluate Options / Shortlist Options / Change Management

## Input Artifacts

Shortlisted option description, stakeholder analysis, cultural analysis.

## Output Artifacts

Force-Field Diagram mapping driving forces and restraining forces, with force-mitigation strategy.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Sponsor, Change Manager, Key Stakeholders.

## Prerequisites

Definition of a specific proposed change option.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

State the proposed change option in the center.
2. Identify driving forces (+ favoring change, e.g. regulator pressure, customer demands).
3. Identify restraining forces (- opposing change, e.g. tight budget, manager hostility).
4. Estimate the relative strength of each force.
5. Formulate actions to weaken negative forces and leverage positive forces.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What forces are pushing this change forward? What forces are resisting or blocking it? How powerful is each force? How can we weaken the negative forces?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Analyzing an IT upgrade option: identifying positive forces (regulator pressure, CEO enthusiasm) vs negative forces (poor financial climate, CFO hostility), leading to strategies to engage the CFO.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Honest identification of negative cultural/political restraining forces alongside positive drivers.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Ignoring powerful negative stakeholder forces; assuming positive forces will automatically overcome negative resistance without targeted action.

## When NOT to Use

When selecting purely technical algorithms where human/organizational forces are absent.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Kurt Lewin's Change Model, Risk Analysis, Stakeholder Management Planning.

## Supported Decisions

Option selection, change management plan formulation, risk mitigation strategy.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Sentiment analysis systems evaluate project communications to identify and rate emerging negative organizational forces automatically.
