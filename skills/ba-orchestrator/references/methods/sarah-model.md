# SARAH Model

`T8 Change Deployment and Solution Evaluation › 8.2 People and Learning`

**Also described as:** The SARAH model

## Problem Solved

Maps the emotional journey individuals experience during major change (Shock -> Anger -> Rejection -> Acceptance -> Hope), guiding empathetic change support strategies.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Manage Change / People Change

## Input Artifacts

Stakeholder Analysis, Change impact assessment, communication plan.

## Output Artifacts

Stakeholder Emotional Support Strategy / Timeline.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Change Manager, Line Managers, Affected Staff.

## Prerequisites

Announcement or implementation of significant business change.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Recognize initial Shock upon change announcement.
2. Empathize with resulting Anger directed at change/analysts.
3. Patiently manage Rejection stage without overreacting or forcing rational arguments.
4. Support staff as they reach Acceptance.
5. Leverage dawning Hope to gather user suggestions for future enhancements.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What emotional stage is this stakeholder currently experiencing? How can we support them through Anger/Rejection without overreacting? Are they ready for training to reach Acceptance?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA encounters user anger and rejection during a new system rollout, remaining patient and empathetic until staff complete training, reach Acceptance, and suggest new ideas (Hope).

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Demonstrated empathy and patience during early negative emotional stages; successful progression of staff to Acceptance and Hope.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Arguing rationally with stakeholders experiencing emotional Anger/Rejection; expecting instant enthusiasm without allowing staff to process the emotional curve.

## When NOT to Use

For minor, unnoticeable system tweaks that trigger no emotional impact on workers.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Kurt Lewin's Change Model, Conscious Competence Model, Kotter's 8-Step Change.

## Supported Decisions

Change communication timing, training plan scheduling, stakeholder support strategy.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Sentiment-analysis AI engines evaluate employee feedback channels during rollout to map staff distribution across the SARAH curve in real-time.
