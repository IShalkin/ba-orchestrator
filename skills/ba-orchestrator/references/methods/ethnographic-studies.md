# Ethnographic Studies

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.7 Observation and Immersion`

**Also described as:** Ethnographic studies

## Problem Solved

Immerses the analyst in the working culture over an extended period to understand social context, implicit norms, and naturalistic working behaviors.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Elicitation

## Input Artifacts

Research objectives, environmental checklist, field notebooks.

## Output Artifacts

Ethnographic field notes, cultural landscape analysis, validated requirements sanity check.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Ethnographer/Researcher), Business Community Members.

## Prerequisites

Organizational approval for extended immersion; researcher objectivity.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Define scope and obtain access to the study area.
2. Immense analyst in the environment over days/weeks.
3. Observe processes, physical layouts, relationships, hierarchy, and informal behaviors.
4. Record comprehensive naturalistic field notes.
5. Analyze notes for cultural patterns, wastes, and underlying needs.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

How do people interact here? What behaviors are rewarded or frowned upon? What informal workarounds exist in this culture?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA spends two weeks immersed in a hospital ward observing nurse-doctor interactions, discovering that paper notes are preferred over mobile screens due to sterilization habits.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Grounded, unbiased naturalistic observations that reflect real-world execution rather than idealized procedures.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Imposing researcher bias or pre-conceived judgments; allowing formal pre-defined checklists to blind the analyst to unexpected cultural dynamics; taking too long without delivering actionable insights.

## When NOT to Use

When rapid requirements delivery is required on tight project deadlines with low budget.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Shadowing, Contextual Inquiry, Personas, Cultural Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Change management strategy, user adoption planning, workplace design, software UI/UX architecture.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

NLP agents analyze unstructured field logs and communication channels to discover sub-cultural patterns and operational friction points automatically.
