# Fishbone Diagram

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.2 Root Cause Analysis`

**Also described as:** Fishbone diagrams

## Problem Solved

Identifies and categorizes the root causes of a specific business problem or undesirable effect, reducing superficial problem solving that only targets symptoms.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Analysis / Documenting Results

## Input Artifacts

Problem statement, investigation findings, workshop inputs.

## Output Artifacts

Fishbone (Cause-and-Effect) Diagram mapping root causes into major categories.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Facilitator), SMEs, Business Users, Operations Leads.

## Prerequisites

A clear, agreed definition of the problem/effect placed at the 'head' of the fish.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Write the problem statement in the box at the 'head'.
2. Draw the main backbone and diagonal category spines (e.g. 6 Ps or 4 Ms).
3. Brainstorm specific causes and attach them as bones to the category spines.
4. Analyze secondary causes attached to primary bones.
5. Identify common or high-impact root causes.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What is the specific undesirable effect? What People, Process, Place, or Technology factors contribute to this? Why did that primary cause happen?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Analyzing 'Poor system availability' using the 6 Ps (People, Place, Processes, Physical evidence, Product, Performance measures) reveals offshore time zone lags and unclear SLAs as key root causes.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Distinguishes checked facts from believed facts/prejudices; identifies true root causes rather than symptoms.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Confusing symptoms with root causes; relying on unverified assumptions/opinions ('believed facts') without factual data verification.

## When NOT to Use

When the problem is simple, single-factor, and obvious without requiring multi-category diagnostic analysis.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys), Mind Maps, Rich Pictures.

## Supported Decisions

Process improvement priorities, problem resolution strategy, business case problem statement.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Diagnostic AI analyzes operational incident logs and categorizes failure root causes onto an automated Fishbone diagram.
