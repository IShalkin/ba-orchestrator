# Paraphrased Solution Review

`T7 Validation and Acceptance › 7.1 Reviews and Inspections`

**Also described as:** Paraphrased Solution Review (Participant Paraphrasing)

## Problem Solved

Prevents passive, superficial document sign-offs where stakeholders approve solution specifications without truly reading or understanding them.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Validation / Specification

## Input Artifacts

Draft solution document, use cases, screen wireframes, or business requirements specification.

## Output Artifacts

Validated solution document, log of identified misunderstandings and corrections.

## Participants & Roles

Business Analyst (facilitator), Product Stakeholders, Process Workers, Solution Team Representatives.

## Prerequisites

Completed draft of the solution document or functional specifications.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Distribute the solution document to participants prior to the review session.
2. Convene the review walkthrough session.
3. Instead of the analyst reading or paraphrasing the document, assign specific sections to individual stakeholder participants.
4. Have the participant paraphrase the section back to the group in their own words, explaining their understanding of how it works.
5. Evaluate the paraphrased explanation against the analyst's intent.
6. If the participant's understanding is correct and accepted, validate the section.
7. If the participant's understanding exposes a document ambiguity or incorrect logic, update the solution document immediately.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

• In your own words, how will this section of the system process your daily work?
• What do you expect to happen when you click this option based on what is written here?
• Does this written flow match how your department intends to operate?

## Minimal Realistic Example

During a review of an A/P voucher requirement, instead of reading section 4.1, the analyst asks Clerk Charley to explain it. Charley says: 'This means I have to sign in twice.' The analyst realizes the written text was ambiguous regarding single sign-on and corrects the requirement on the spot.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

100% of reviewed sections are verbalized correctly by stakeholders in their own operational context, proving true mutual understanding prior to formal approval.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

The analyst reading the document aloud line-by-line while participants passively nod; accepting written sign-offs without conducting active comprehension checks.

## When NOT to Use

For pure technical infrastructure specifications where business stakeholders have no operational interaction.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Structured Walkthroughs, Peer Reviews, Interactive Prototyping, Acceptance Test-Driven Reviews.

## Supported Decisions

Formal baseline approval, sign-off on requirements, readiness for technical design.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

An AI assistant can generate simplified, role-specific summary prompts or interactive scenario quizzes from complex requirements documents to test stakeholder comprehension before formal sign-off.
