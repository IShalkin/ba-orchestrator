# Business Activity Modelling

`T4 Modelling and Analysis › 4.1 Business Processes and User Work`

**Also described as:** Business activity modelling

## Problem Solved

Creates a conceptual model of activities required to fulfill a specific stakeholder worldview, independent of current operational execution, reducing legacy process bias during gap analysis.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Consider Perspectives / Analysis / Specification

## Input Artifacts

CATWOE outputs, Root Definition statement.

## Output Artifacts

Business Activity Model (BAM) diagram showing Plan, Enable, Do, Monitor, and Control activities with logical dependency links.

## Participants & Roles

BA, Key Stakeholder / SME group.

## Prerequisites

Agreed CATWOE analysis and Root Definition.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify 1-2 core 'Doing' (D) primary task activities.
2. Identify required 'Enabling' (E) activities for resources.
3. Identify 'Planning' (P) activities that set performance targets.
4. Identify 'Monitoring' (M) activities to track Doing tasks against targets.
5. Add 'Control' (C) action activities fed by Monitoring outputs.
6. Draw logical dependency arrows.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What are the primary doing tasks? What resources must be enabled before doing can happen? What planning sets targets? What monitors performance? What control action occurs if targets are missed?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A retail BAM shows 'Sell clothes' (Doing), enabled by 'Design clothes' and 'Acquire stores', planned by 'Define customer profile', monitored by 'Monitor sales profits', and controlled by 'Take control action'.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Model is conceptual (what, not how); includes Plan, Enable, Do, Monitor, and Control activity types; contains unique activity definitions.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Modeling current physical reality ('as is') instead of a conceptual ideal model; adding process flow arrows or swimsuit lanes prematurely; creating too many 'Doing' tasks.

## When NOT to Use

When analyzing low-level physical IT system interface interactions or detailed step-by-step procedure flowcharts.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

BAM, Conceptual Model, Business Process Modelling, Gap Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Gap analysis target setting, target operating model design, business process scope boundary.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI modeling tools auto-generate candidate BAM activity structures from input CATWOE root definitions, verifying logical activity dependencies.
