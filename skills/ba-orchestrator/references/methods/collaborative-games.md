# Collaborative Games

`T2 Situation Investigation and Elicitation › 2.6 Group and Creative Sessions`

## Problem Solved

Reduces stakeholder communication barriers, hidden assumptions, and reserved participation by using structured game play to foster collaboration and creative insight.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

elicitation / analysis / Discovery

## Input Artifacts

Elicitation topic/problem statement, game rules, physical/virtual collaboration tools (sticky notes, boards).

## Output Artifacts

Group-generated feature lists, affinity maps, visual product boxes, identified hidden assumptions.

## Participants & Roles

Facilitator (neutral), BA, Stakeholders, SMEs, End Users, Developers.

## Prerequisites

Clear objective for the game session and a comfortable, safe collaborative environment.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Select an appropriate game (e.g., Product Box, Affinity Map, Fishbowl) matching the objective.
2. Explain the purpose and process/rules of the game to participants.
3. Opening step: engage participants and begin rapid idea generation.
4. Exploration step: engage participants in testing, connecting, and experimenting with ideas.
5. Closing step: evaluate, synthesize, and prioritize the ideas generated.
6. Record and document the outcomes for follow-up decision making.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

"If this product were sold in a retail store, what key features would be printed on its box?", "How do these features group together naturally?", "What assumptions are we making?"

## Minimal Realistic Example

Participants design a physical 'Product Box' for an internal IT portal, revealing that 'Instant Chat Support' is considered the top selling feature by end users.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Active participation across reserved and vocal team members; clear identification of shared themes and hidden perspectives.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Perceiving games as silly/unproductive due to poor framing; failing to synthesize game results into actionable business analysis artifacts.

## When NOT to Use

In rigid, highly formal corporate cultures hostile to game play, or when quick deterministic technical decisions are needed.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Alternatives include Brainstorming, Focus Groups, Workshops, and Mind Mapping.

## Supported Decisions

Product feature scoping, assumption validation, team consensus building.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Virtual collaboration boards driven by AI can auto-cluster sticky notes during affinity mapping games and sentiment-analyze participant discussions in real time.
