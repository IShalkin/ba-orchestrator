# Options Identification

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.4 Solution Options and Feasibility`

**Also described as:** Options identification

## Problem Solved

Generates a comprehensive longlist of potential business and technical solution options, preventing premature commitment to a single unexamined solution.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Evaluate Options / Identify Options

## Input Artifacts

Gap Analysis results, problem statement, business strategy, MOST analysis.

## Output Artifacts

Options Longlist covering business and technical possibilities (including 'Do Nothing').

## Participants & Roles

BA, Sponsor, Business Owner, Technical Architect, SMEs.

## Prerequisites

Clear definition of the business problem and change gap.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Convene creative workshop with key stakeholders.
2. Brainstorm business solution options (e.g. outsource, change strategy, manual process).
3. Brainstorm technical solution options (e.g. buy package, build bespoke, enhance existing).
4. Ensure 'Do Nothing' option is included.
5. Compile un-filtered longlist of options.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What are all the different ways we could solve this problem? What if we bought a package? What if we outsourced? What happens if we do nothing?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A firm facing legacy system obsolescence brainstorms a longlist:
1. Buy package, 2. Enhance existing, 3. Revert to manual, 4. Outsource, 5. Exit business, 6. Share competitor system, 7. Do nothing.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Broad longlist covering diverse business and technical dimensions without premature filtering or dismissals.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Prematurely rejecting 'off-the-wall' options before evaluation; blindly accepting the sponsor's pet solution without generating alternative options; omitting 'Do Nothing'.

## When NOT to Use

When the solution option is strictly dictated by mandatory statutory legal compliance with zero alternative choices.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Brainstorming, Hothousing, SWOT Analysis.

## Supported Decisions

Business case scope options, solution approach selection.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

Generative AI parses the problem statement and industry benchmarks to generate a broad candidate list of innovative business and technical options.
