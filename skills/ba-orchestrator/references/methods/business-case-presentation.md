# Business Case Presentation

`T1 Strategy Analysis and Justification › 1.5 Business Case`

**Also described as:** Business case presentation

## Problem Solved

Delivers a short, sharp, persuasive executive briefing to decision-makers, summarizing the business case recommendations and securing approval.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Evaluate Options / Present Business Case

## Input Artifacts

Business Case Report, executive stakeholder profiles.

## Output Artifacts

Slide Deck / Executive Presentation, approved business case decision.

## Participants & Roles

BA (Presenter), Project Sponsor, Executive Decision-Makers (Audience).

## Prerequisites

Completed Business Case Report; booked executive presentation slot.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Understand executive audience interests and available time slot (e.g. 15 mins).
2. Create minimal, highly visual slides (<5 slides).
3. Start with an impactful opening statement ('Start with a bang!').
4. Present main issues and solution option.
5. State explicit recommendations and required decision ('End on a high').
6. Rehearse with colleagues acting as critical managers.
7. Deliver presentation and answer questions.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What headline message will hook these executives immediately? What decision do I need from them today? What tough questions will they ask?

## Minimal Realistic Example

A BA opens a 15-minute board presentation stating: 'This proposal will save our firm £12m/year in operating expenses', presenting 4 visual slides and securing project sign-off.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Delivered strictly within time limits; concise visual slides; active engagement and confident handling of executive questions.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Reading the text of the report word-for-word off dense slides ('death by PowerPoint'); taking too many slides; lacking rehearsed answers to tough financial/risk questions.

## When NOT to Use

When decision-makers require only a formal written report submission without a live meeting.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Business Case Report Creation, 4As Communication Model.

## Supported Decisions

Project authorization, budget sign-off, option selection.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI presentation tools auto-generate visual slide decks directly from written business case reports and simulate executive Q&A sessions for rehearsal.
