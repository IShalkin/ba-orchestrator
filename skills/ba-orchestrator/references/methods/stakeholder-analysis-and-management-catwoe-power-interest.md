# Stakeholder Analysis and Management (CATWOE, Power/Interest)

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.2 Stakeholder Analysis and Mapping`

**Also described as:** Stakeholder Analysis and Management (CATWOE & Power/Interest Grid)

## Problem Solved

Reduces political resistance, conflicting perspectives, poor buy-in, and communication breakdowns during change initiatives.

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Discovery / Elicitation / Analysis / Change Management

## Input Artifacts

Stakeholder list/wheel, project objectives, interview findings, organisational charts.

## Output Artifacts

Power/Interest Grid, Stakeholder Management Plan, CATWOE definitions, Business Activity Models (BAM), RACI/RASCI matrix.

## Participants & Roles

Sponsor, BA, Project Manager, Key Stakeholders.

## Prerequisites

Identification of project stakeholders across internal and external categories.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Identify stakeholders using the Stakeholder Wheel.
2. Analyse stakeholders' power and interest.
3. Map stakeholders onto the Power/Interest Grid.
4. Formulate stakeholder management strategies (e.g., Key Player, Keep Satisfied).
5. Elicit individual stakeholder perspectives using CATWOE.
6. Build Business Activity Models (BAM) for contrasting perspectives.
7. Resolve conflicts to build a consensus BAM.
8. Define stakeholder responsibilities using RACI/RASCI.

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

Who is impacted by this change? Who holds the power to block or support it? What is the stakeholder's World View (Weltanschauung)? Who is responsible, accountable, consulted, or informed?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Resolving a clash between a Sales Manager (wanting aggressive telesales) and a Customer Services Manager (wanting relationship management) by mapping their CATWOEs and building a consensus BAM.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Consensus or owner-approved BAM, clear RACI matrix, implemented stakeholder engagement plan, reduction in resistance.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Treating stakeholder groups as homogeneous, placing stakeholders on the grid where they 'should' be rather than where they 'are', ignoring low-power groups who can aggregate power.

## When NOT to Use

When a change is strictly mechanical, non-contentious, and affects no human roles or responsibilities.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Soft Systems Methodology (SSM), Persona Analysis, RACI/RASCI, Mind Mapping, Rich Pictures.

## Supported Decisions

Scope, priority, change management, governance structure.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI can perform social network analysis and sentiment analysis on stakeholder communications to dynamically update the Power/Interest grid and flag emerging conflicts.
