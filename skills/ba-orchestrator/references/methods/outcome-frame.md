# Outcome Frame

`T3 Stakeholders › 3.3 Engagement, Conflict and Negotiation`

## Problem Solved

Shifts planning and negotiation mindsets from problem/blame orientation to a positive, future-focused outcome orientation (What do I want?, How will I know?, What resources?).

## BA Lifecycle Phase

Manage Change / People Change / Planning / Negotiation

## Input Artifacts

Problem statements, negotiation contexts, stakeholder disputes.

## Output Artifacts

Outcome Plan (Desired Outcome, Rationale, Timing, Verification criteria, Current state, Resource needs, Action steps, Impact assessment).

## Participants & Roles

BA, Stakeholders, Negotiating Parties.

## Prerequisites

Identification of a desired goal, change objective, or negotiation dispute.

## Step-by-Step Procedure

Answer: What outcome do I want? 2. Why do I want it? 3. When do I want it? 4. How will I know I achieved it? 5. Where am I now? 6. What resources do I need? 7. What action steps are required? 8. What else is affected?

## Analyst Questions & Prompts

What specific positive outcome do we want to achieve? How will we verify success? What resources are needed? What are the step-by-step actions?

## Minimal Realistic Example

Resolving a requirements dispute between two leads: using Outcome Frame questions to shift focus from past arguments to agreeing on a consensus outcome within 5 days using data analysis.

## Quality Criteria & Verification

Future-focused, positive outcome definition; explicit verification criteria; actionable step-by-step execution plan.

## Errors & Anti-Patterns

Falling back into problem-blame thinking ('Who caused this bug?'); defining vague outcomes without verification criteria.

## When NOT to Use

When performing forensic root-cause auditing of past financial fraud where identifying past problem culpability is mandatory.

## Alternatives & Complementary Methods

Outcome Thinking, NLP Planning, Principled Negotiation, SMART Objectives.

## Supported Decisions

Negotiation resolution, change initiative goal definition, project planning.

## AI & Agentic Adaptation

AI coaching tools guide analysts through interactive Outcome Frame prompts during dispute resolution or project planning sessions.
