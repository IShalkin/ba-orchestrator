# WF-E1 — Initiative Discovery (idea → agreed problem, scope, stakeholders, high-level needs)

**Use when:** a new initiative, product idea or request arrives and nobody has agreed yet what the problem is, who is involved, or what success looks like.
**Composes:** T2 (frame, diagnose, elicit) + T3 (stakeholders) + T1 (goals and measures) + T4 (goal model, light).
**Deliverables:** a problem statement, root causes, a context diagram, a stakeholder register and map, an expectations log (when there is a client or sponsor), business objectives with KPIs, an elicitation plan with instruments, a goal model, and an open-questions list.

## Waves
| Wave | Executors (parallel inside a wave) | Depends on |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `situation-investigation-and-problem-analysis` · `background-study` · `stakeholder-identification-and-analysis` (+ `onion-model` at standard/deep depth) | research |
| 2 | `root-cause-analysis` · `context-diagram` · `power-interest-grid` · `business-objectives-vision-and-scope` · `client-expectations-capture-and-management` (when there is a client or sponsor) | 1 |
| 3 | `critical-success-factors-and-kpis` · `goal-modelling-kaos-gore` (light: goals and refinements only) · `information-gathering-plan` | 2 |
| 4 | `interviews` (guide per stakeholder class) · `requirements-workshops` (agenda) · `storyboards-and-scenarios` (when users need concrete pictures) | 3 |

Switching rules: as in WF-T2 and WF-T3 (`project-blastoff` replaces wave 1's problem framing at a formal kick-off; `rich-pictures` replaces `context-diagram` for messy socio-political situations; `impact-mapping` is added in wave 3 when goals must link to actors and deliverables).

## Research questions (seed)
Combine the seeds of WF-T2, WF-T3 and WF-T1, focused on: the origin of the request, evidence of the problem, affected roles, stated goals, and constraints.

## Gates
1. After wave 2: confirm the problem statement, scope boundary and key stakeholders with the user. Show the expectations that fall outside the scope boundary next to it, so that the user decides how each one is handled.
2. After wave 3: confirm the objectives and KPIs. They become the success criteria for the later workflows.
